Potions

Potions are consumable items that provide effects. Potions can be drunk as an action.
Potions can also be thrown (4 tiles), creatures can do a Reflex saving throw to dodge thrown potions. Drinking multiple potions with the same effect does not stack the effect, but does extend the duration to the last one drunk
