Combat Maneuvers

Combat Maneuvers are actions you take in combat. Strike and basic combat maneuvers can be done by anyone, while more advanced ones require specific feats. Hostile actions increase your multiple attack penalty or MAP. Each level of MAP gives you a stacking -5 penalty for future attack rolls during the turn.

Combat Maneuvers traits
Draining; Costs a focus point.
Blade Art; You can do a limited amount of Blade Arts per day.
