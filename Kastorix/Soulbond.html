Soulbond Rules

You have a soulbond, a bond to a magical creature that allows communication, sharing of magic and other benefits. To acquire a soulbond you have to take Soulbond advantage and performing a soulbonding ritual in the game.
The power of creatures you can soulbond with depends on your level, with more powerful creatures requiring higher levels.

You can spend one action to give a general command to your soulbond, such as “attack that target” or “follow me”, which it will follow until it gets new commands. You can also spend one or more actions to command it more specifically. Soulbonded creatures understand you and will generally follow your commands, but can refuse orders that would needlessly endanger them or go against their will. You can aura block for your soulbond using your own mana. You can only have one soulbond at a time, and if your soulbond dies you take 2d6 vital damage.

There are arcane feats to enhance your soulbonded creature and share some of its power, as well as teleport and shield it.
