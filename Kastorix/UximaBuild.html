Uxima Build Guide

How to set up an archmage

Overview

There are a lot of options when it comes to building a character in Kastorix, and perhaps it might seem overwhelming at first. This is a guide set up to go through building a character, as we build one of the five archmages on Ishyin council, Uxima of Colours

You might be building a similar mage or a completely different character, but hopefully this guide will still be helpful, in how and what decisions to consider.

Build Goals

Uxima is a naga archmage, one of the most powerful mages in the world and someone completely obsessed with magic. Perfect as jumping off point for how to build a character in a world built on magic.

As a character she has no specific elemental focus, using any and all aspects that fit the situation. She will pick up diverse aspects of magic, gadgets, soul magic, dimensionalism and more. This guide will so far only cover level one, but I might extend it to further levels later as Uxima is probably around level 19 in lore.

Basic Info

Uxima is a Naga and a nerdy one. Choosing from the array, we are setting her INT as the highest stat (3), DEX as second highest (1), WIL at a moderate value (0) and dumping PHY (-1). Uxima didn’t excel at gym class.

From being a naga she gets bonuses to grappling and natural claw weapon, neither of which will be particularly useful for her. More useful though is the 2 extra spells learned from magical talents and slither ability allowing her to move 2 tiles when using the step action.

Background and Advantage

The first two choices we are picking for her are Background and Advantage. For Background Uxima will have the scholar background as that certainly fits, this gives her proficiency in arcana, nature or history, 5 gold and the dedicated student feat, which gives her an arcane feat. She is picking nature here as she has other sources to give her arcana. For advantage, Uxima doesn’t have an innate aspect like soulsight or an attunement, instead she spends her time studying magic and picks up the arcane training advantage. This makes her trained in arcana and gives her two more arcane feats. Yes this means she has five arcane feats to choose at level one. That's a lot of choices and a lot of magic, fitting our scholarly snake.

From the dedicated student arcane feat Uxima picks up spellcraft scholar increasing her knowledge of arcana to expert and giving her a bonus to identify spells. The two arcane feats she gets from arcane training can be level 2, letting her pick up studied ahead allowing her to learn two adept spells (with restrictions) at level one. The feat is used for gadget crafting to craft fun gadgets and become trained in crafting.

Other Feats

Her level one ancestry feat will be to pick up Sha’des’ka Practitioner, and therefore the duelist general feat, allowing her to open initiative with a quick spell or step. Finally from her remaining arcane and general feats she picks five metamagics with metamagic novice, a few extra bonuses from tools of the trade and an eye for danger from alert.

This leaves her with expertise in arcana, and trained with crafting, mana sensing (from tools of the trade), nature and perception. She then picks up training in deception, investigation and sleight of hand as her free skill training from her intelligence (number of skills equal to int). The sheet currently lacks a reminder to pick these skills, but that is something that I want to add later.

Spells and the rest

Finally Uxima picks her spells, and selects a mix of aspects and types. She has ferrokinesis, zap and heat as damage spells, magnetic rebound for defense, ascend for mobility and blinding flash and slip to disadvantage her foes. For her two studied ahead spells she selects orbiting earths and teleport, giving her an offensive/defensive spell and the only way to teleport at level one. She also gets to pick six whole metamagics and picks up careful, close-range, sculpted, obscure, channelled and expand spell for a range of modifications when she casts spells.

Uxima doesn’t really use weapons or combat manoeuvres, so don’t need to look at them nor is she wielding any armor. She also has no save proficiencies which she may find to be a problem if enemies can overcome her spell barrages. She does have gadget crafting so at every day start she can pick two gadgets to craft (not included on the character sheet yet).
