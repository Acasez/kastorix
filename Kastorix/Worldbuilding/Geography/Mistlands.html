Mistlands

Overview

The Mistlands are a region of southeastern Meibor characterized for ever-present mist that covers the land. The Mistlands holds few settlements, though it said that there are large ruins inside. It is also home to many dangerous creatures, and a group of outlaws called the Mist Raiders.

Geography

The Mistlands borders Deshir to the east and Ganderei to the north, as well as easternmost edge of the Everglow Mountains
