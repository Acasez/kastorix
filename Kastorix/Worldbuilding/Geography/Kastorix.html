Kastorix World

Overview

Kastorix is a large, blue moon with multiple continents, orbiting the gas giant of Teivis. Kastorix is home to many sapient species and civilizations, the most advanced of which who are in the middle of a industrial revolution. Kastorix is smaller than most planets, but has a dense core of adamantium that gives it gravity similar to many terrestrial planets. It is one of the largest moons of the many orbiting Teivis.

30 Years ago Kastorix was thrown into chaos when an event known as The Breach happened, and a portal to Teivis opened on the northern continent of Avijor. From this portal a large amounts of the so-called Void Creatures emergent and quickly conquered most of the continent. Now only the The Draconium remains on the Avijor, holding the creatures at bay with dragonfire and steel.

Geography

On the southern continent of Meibor the largest nation of Theze has recently had a regime change, creating a parliament for the first time. Next to it sits Ishyin Academy, a which has taken the influx of mages, scholars and new students in stride. Meibor also has the ancient desert nation of Jadiar, known to be haven for spellcasters, the republic of Thune which produces a decent amount of the worlds airships and a many traditional Yirte communities and a host of other nations.

West of Meibor lies Santsim, the smallest continent. Meibor has the ancient nation of El-Shavri and the newly unified federation Glarnast, which are both world powers. It also has the nomadic tribes of Quarthax on its southern shores and commune of Tharn in the north, and a few other nations.

Much of Kastorix is covered by a water, most of which lies in a great ocean called the grand sea. There is an large archipelago known as The Shattered Isles in the far east and numerous smaller islands spread around. Kastorix also has a vast amount of underground caves, connected in a giant cave system called The Caverns Below. These caves are inhabited by the bat-like vesper which have two large competing nations the Emerald Empire and the Crimson Spire there.
