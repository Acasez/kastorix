Jadieran Desert

The Jadieran desert is a large desert on the west coast of Meibor. Most of the desert lies in the nation of Jadiar. It's famous for its very high temperatures, which comes at least in part from the rare Ember Dust contained within.

Geography

The Jadieran desert lies on the central western coast of Meibor, west of the Everglow Mountains. Most of the desert is sparsely populated, barring the Jadieran capital of Shanar and numerous cities on the coast.
