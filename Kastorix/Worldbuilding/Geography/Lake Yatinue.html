Lake Yatinue

Overview

Lake Yatinue is one of the largest lakes in Kastorix, located in the southern part of Najanova Jungle, in the center of Meibor. Lake Yatinue is fed by many mountain streams that run into it from the neighboring Everglow Mountains. The Yearning runs out from the lake towards the northeastern coast.

Geography

Lake Yatinue is the lake by area in Kastorix, at 175,000 km². The lake is fed by many streams that come down from the nearby mountains in the Everglow mountain chain. The lake runs of into the The Yearning river, which runs north out to grand sea.

Biodiversity

The lake is large enough to have its own ecosystem and climate. There are many species of fish and amphibians in the lake.

The lake is also known for the many water elementals that live there. Many of them feature in traditional naga myths, though they may occasionally prove dangerous as some elementals will try to drown travelers.
Some unique flora and fauna that live near the lake are.
- Lunar Lilies: A rare, bioluminescent flower that blooms only during full moons, floating on the lake’s surface and glowing with a soft, pale light. The lilies are prized by alchemists and herbalists for their use in potions of clarity and calming.
- Gloom Crocodiles: A dangerous predator, gloom crocodiles can see through any darkness and can spread a cloud of darkness from specialized scales on their back. They are particularly common on the western edge, where there is a small marshland, with bogs and unique plants.
- Myrkra Fish: A species of deep-dwelling fish that can change their coloration to blend into the dark depths. Myrkra Fish are known to be incredibly elusive, but their scales are highly valued for creating enchanted armor.
- Shavarazen, are a rare variant of mist elementals that live on the lakes water. They are said to sometimes point towards lost treasure.

Settlements

There are two nations that border the lake, Narrei on the west and south coast and Ellios on the eastern coast. The lake and the Yearning river acts as a trading hub, connecting the interior of the jungle to the coast. Ellios, while landlocked without coast has a sizeable harbor and merchant fleet that trades out from the lake and connected rivers. Most of the cities and towns in both nations sit on the lake shore.

Narrei's coast features multiple floating gardens that grow many different crops in the lakes fertile waters. The lake also has a fishing industry with many boats, harvesting the unique fish that thrive in its climate.
