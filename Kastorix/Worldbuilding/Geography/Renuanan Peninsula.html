Renuanan Peninsula

Overview

The Renuanan Peninsula is a region in the northern part of Meibor. While most of the of it lies in Renue, the northeastern tip houses the famous Ishyin Academy and the southern third part of lies in Theze and Aldarna. Most of is covered in flat plains, though the central part has regions of hills and a dormant volcano
