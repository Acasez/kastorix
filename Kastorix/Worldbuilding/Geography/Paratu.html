Paratu

Overview

Paratu is the larger of Te-Ohuvi main islands, spanning approximately 155,000 km². It is home its the capital city, Telavaste, which is renowned for its impressive coastal fortifications, vibrant marketplaces, and the grand palace of the Troll King. The island features dense tropical forests, rolling hills, and a series of inland lagoons that are vital for the local fishing industry.
