The Deeper Reaches

The Deeper Reaches is the colloquial name for the deeper areas of The Caverns Below where the intense heat makes life hard and the high mana concentration allows creates many strange magical creatures. Exploration of the deeper reaches is difficult and dangerous, even for seasoned explorers and experienced mages. The high concentration of mana and magical materials makes such exploration valuable however.

Geography

Due to dangerous environments it's unclear how large The Deeper Reaches are and how deep they go. Explorers have spoken of vast crystal caves, lava lakes and strange ruins.

Biodiversity

There are no recognized sapients living in the deeper reaches, but vesper explorers have spoken of an mysterious insectoid species wielding forged weapons wandering the depths. Nicknamed the Kilkaz for the clicking sounds they make, its unclear how they survive the depths or why they stay there. The Kilkaz are a beetle-like insectoid species with orange carapaces and prominent antennae. They appear to have some form of civilization as they have been seen wielding metallic weapons.

Beside the Kilkaz, The Deeper Reaches also has multiple dangerous and strange creatures like the Yasailir.
