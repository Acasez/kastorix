Sezei Highlands

Overview

The Sezei Highlands is hilly peninsula in northern Meibor, north of the Najanova Jungle. The highlands are slightly more temperate than the nearby jungle and windy, with maritime winds from three directions. At the tip of peninsula is Meibor's northmost point and the location of the large city of Sezei-Lar. The highlands lie entirely within the kingdom of Theze.
