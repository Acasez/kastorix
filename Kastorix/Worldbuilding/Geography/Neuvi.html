Neuvi

Overview

Neuvi is the second island of Te-Ohuvi at around 140,000 km² in size, lies southeast of Paratu. This island is more mountainous, dominated by the imposing Hea'ran volcano. Neuvi's rugged terrain and fertile volcanic soil have fostered a unique ecosystem, including rare flora used in traditional troll medicine. The island’s volcanic activity also contributes to the rich mineral deposits found in the surrounding waters, which the trolls have long exploited for crafting.
