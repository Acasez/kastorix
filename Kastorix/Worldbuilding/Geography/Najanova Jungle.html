Najanova Jungle

The Najanova Jungle, also known as the Meiboran Jungle is the largest jungle on Kastorix, covering much of northern and central Meibor. It spans over thousands of kilometers, with a thick undergrowth and a labyrinth of rivers and streams that feed the jungle's immense biodiversity. The jungle is home to a rich biodiversity of flora and fauna, both magical and mundane. The jungle includes Kastorix's biggest river The Yearning and one of it's biggest lakes, Lake Yatinue.

It is also the ancestral homeland of the nagas, who have lived here for millennia, and the birthplace of many of their traditions and stories. The nations of Ellios and Narrei lie entirely within the jungle, while Aldarna, Theze, Heksan and Ganderei include part of the jungle within its borders. The jungle also contains multiple traditional yirte communities.

Geography

The Najanova Jungle is a living, breathing labyrinth of tangled roots, towering trees, and thick underbrush. It stretches from the Everglow Mountains in the south and southwest, which act as a natural border between the jungle and the cooler temperate forests of southern Meibor. To the east, the jungle fades into a mixed temperate woodland, while to the north and west, the jungle gives way to flatlands and open fields.

Deforestation over the centuries has reduced the size of the jungle significantly, especially in the north where settlements and farmland have been established. However, much of the central and southern jungle remains untouched, preserved by natural barriers, fierce wildlife, and deep-rooted traditions that forbid excessive exploitation.

**Notable Regions**

  • Lake Yatinue: A massive freshwater lake, surrounded by towering trees whose branches cast deep shadows over the water's surface. The lake is fed by countless streams from the Everglow Mountains. It's home to magical creatures and holds special significance for the nagas. It is also rumored to have deep underwater ruins of ancient civilizations.
  • The Yearning: The largest river in Kastorix, flowing from the Lake Yatinue through the jungle and out to see. The river is essential to the jungle’s ecosystem, providing water to countless species and serving as a major transportation route for locals. At it's mouth sits Naga-Lar, an ancient trade city, today part of kingdom of Theze.

Biodiversity

The Najanova Jungle is teeming with life, from the tiniest insects to the largest predators, many of which have magical properties or connections. Its dense foliage provides shelter for rare species found nowhere else on Kastorix. Some examples include:
- Siphriax: Giant, semi-intelligent wolf-sized social spiders that build elaborate web cities high in the canopy. These webs are tough, magic resistant and waterproof, serving as walkways, traps and defenses. The Siphriax are known to communicate through vibrations and gestures, and can sometimes agree to trade with locals.
- Jungle Hydras: A dangerous species of multi-headed reptiles with rapid regeneration abilities. They are highly territorial and have been hunted to near extinction, though they remain a significant threat in more remote areas. Their hearts are prized for making extremely valuable potions of regeneration.
- Coatl: Mysterious, wise, and powerful sapient flying snakes. Coatls are revered by many cultures within the jungle.. They rarely interact with other sapient species, though are welcome to those that stumble into their groves.
- Moonleaf Trees: A rare species of tree whose leaves glow faintly with mana during the night. Some nagas consider these trees sacred, and their leaves are used in powerful healing potions. The trees are thought to be connected to the other moons.
- Crystal Raptor: A bird-like predator with wings that shimmer with mana-infused crystals. These agile creatures hunt in packs, using their sharp talons and dazzling plumage to blind and disorient their prey before striking. Their feathers are sought after by artificers and enchanters for creating powerful mana-conducting items.
- Mirestalker Lizard: A semi-aquatic reptilian species that dwells in the murky swamps and rivers of the jungle. They have the ability to camouflage themselves with the environment and can breathe both air and water. Their stealth and speed make them formidable hunters.
