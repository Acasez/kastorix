The Yearning

Overview

The yearning is the biggest river in Kastorix, located in the Najanova Jungle on the continent of Meibor. The Yearning flows from Lake Yatinue out to sea at Naga-Lar. The river spans for over 650 kilometers and flows at a steady pace.

Geography

The yearnings flow shapes much of the eastern past of the Najanova Jungle.
