Crystalline Lake

The crystalline lake is large subterranean sea located in The Caverns Below. It's one of the largest lakes in Kastorix and the largest body of water below ground. The lake gets its name from the many magical glowing crystals that grow around it, shining with soft light that gets reflected by the water. The capital of the Emerald Empire, lies by the lake with many houses being by the lakeshore.

For it's large size, the crystalline lake is quite shallow with few parts over a dozen meters in depth. It still boosts a variety of aquatic species, including several species of fish. The waters are quite clean and reflective, being purified from natural springs and crystal artifice.

Geography

There are many settlements on the side of the lake, the most prominent of which being Emerald Citadel, the capital of the emerald empire. There are many ports on the lake for small fishing boats and barges. Due to the lakes low depth, there is little space for larger boats in it.

Many settlements around the lake are built on fishing and crystal harvesting, with goods being shipped across the lake on barges.

Biodiversity

  • Fish Varieties: The lake is home to several unique species of fish adapted to the crystal-infused waters. Some have bioluminescent scales that shimmer with light from the crystals.
  • Other Fauna: Various types of crustaceans, mollusks, and other aquatic creatures thrive in the clean, reflective waters. These species are often used as food sources for the vespers of nearby settlements.
  • Flora: Some rare types of algae and plants grows in the lake, and is eaten by the its sealife. The emerald empire grows some of it in specialized basins for use in crafting.
