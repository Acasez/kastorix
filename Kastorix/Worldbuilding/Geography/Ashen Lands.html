Ashen Lands

The ashen lands is a unique region in central Santsim famous for its hot temperatures, volcanic activity and many fire elementals. It's a sparsely populated region with many dangers, though also with many valuable minerals to find.

Geography

The ashen lands are located east of El-Shavri, south of Tharn and north of the central highlands. It's a hilly, harsh terrain that's difficult to navigate. It's hotter than it would normally be due to intense fire magic present, which further discourages travel and exploration. The only nation that lays claim to part of the nation is the kingdom of Terill which owns the easternmost part.

Biodiversity

  • Fauna: The ashen lands has little mundane life, as the heat and dangerous environment makes it though to live in. Many fire elementals live in the ashen lands.
  • Flora: Few plants grow in the region. It does however contain rare netherblooms, which thrive in volcanic climates and are use in alchemy.
