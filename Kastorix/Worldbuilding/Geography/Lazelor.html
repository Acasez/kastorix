Lazelor

Overview

Lazelor is a cold, rocky island located south of Santsim. Lazerlors top half is densely vegetated and houses the islands only sapient population. The southern half turns into a barren tundra. Lazelor is known for its Kryorite harvesting.

South Lazelor lies the smaller unpopulated island of Deves, one of the coldest known places on the southern half of Kastorix.
