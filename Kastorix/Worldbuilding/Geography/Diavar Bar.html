Overview

Avijor is the northernmost and largest of the continents on Kastorix. Historically it was composed of many different nations, however after [[The Breach]], only The Draconium remains standing. Before the breach Avijor had a majority [[Humans|human]] population, with a significant amount of [[Gnomes|gnomes]], [[Brannur|brannur]], [[Dragonkin|dragonkin]] and [[Yirte|yirte]]. It also had all of Kastorix's [[Elves|elves]] before they vanished in the breach.
