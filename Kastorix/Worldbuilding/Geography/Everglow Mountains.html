Everglow Mountains

Overview

The Everglow Mountains are a majestic mountain chain that stretches across central Meibor, cutting the continent diagonally in half. This range is the largest on Kastorix, featuring many of the tallest peaks in the world. Starting from the Mistlands in the south and extending to southern Thune, the Everglow Mountains acts as a natural divider between the vast jungle of central-northern Meibor and the colder temperate forests in the south. Much of the snow and rainfall that accumulates in the mountains feeds into the Lake Yatinue, Kastorix's largest lake, which in turn flows out through the jungle and into the sea.

Name and Visual Characteristics

The name "Everglow Mountains" originates from the reflective properties of the mountain surfaces, which make them appear to glow at certain times of day. This phenomenon is particularly striking during sunrise and sunset when the mountains' surfaces catch the light, creating a stunning visual display that can be seen from great distances.

Geography

The Everglow Mountains contain some of the most notable geographical features on Kastorix:
- Heaven's Peak: The tallest mountain on Kastorix, Heaven's Peak stands majestically in the center of the range. Its summit is perpetually covered in snow and ice, making it a formidable challenge for even the most experienced climbers.
- The Silent Mountain: An important site in Vesper mythology, The Silent Mountain is famed for its eerie quietness. Legends say that no sound can be heard at its summit, even during the fiercest storms. In recent times The Silent Mountain has come under disputes, as vast amount of adamantium has been discovered in the mountain.
