Brannur

The brannur are a rare species of robust humanoids, distinguished by their green to gray skin, red eyes, and a single prominent horn on their heads. Known for their formidable combat skills, many brannur have served in battles across the world. Before The Breach, a significant number of them resided in one of the central kingdoms of Avijor. However, compared to gnomes and humans, fewer brannur managed to escape the cataclysmic event.


Brannur

Population: 7 Million
Average Lifespan: 70 years
Size: 1.7 - 2.2 meters
Rarity: Rare

Physical Characteristics

Brannur typically stand between 1.7 and 2.3 meters tall, with well-built, muscular physiques that contribute to their reputation as great fighters. Their skin tones range from green to gray, and their striking red eyes and single horn add to their distinctive appearance.

Society and Culture

The brannur have a rich and proud oral tradition, with stories that trace back to the earliest days of Kastorix. This deep-rooted tradition has fostered a talent and passion for drama, theatre, and sports within brannur society. Their arts tend to be more action-packed compared to those of other species, reflecting their dynamic and vigorous culture.

While traditionally dismissive of wizardry, the brannur hold any attuned born among them in high esteem, providing opportunities for these individuals to rise through the ranks. Despite not being known as inventors, the brannur have shown a remarkable ability to quickly adapt to new innovations in artifice. They have even developed unique styles and weapons specifically designed to combat wizards.

Economy and Craftsmanship

Brannur are skilled blacksmiths and armorers, crafting high-quality weapons and armor that are sought after by warriors across the world. Their economy thrives on trade related to warfare, including the training of soldiers, the forging of arms, and mercenary contracts. They are also known for their expertise in tactics and strategy, often serving as advisors or commanders in foreign armies.

Religion and Beliefs

Brannur worship a pantheon of warrior deities, with rituals and ceremonies centered around combat, honor, and the ancestors who fell in battle. They believe in proving their worth through deeds and valor, and their spiritual practices often involve trials of strength and endurance.

Daily Life and Practices

Education

Education among the brannur focuses on martial training from a young age. Children are taught combat techniques, strategy, and the history of their people, with an emphasis on discipline and physical conditioning. Storytelling is also a key part of their upbringing, as elders pass down the legends of past warriors and battles.

Art and Music

The brannur have a rich and proud oral tradition, with stories that trace back to the earliest days of Kastorix. This deep-rooted tradition has fostered a talent and passion for drama, theater, and sports within brannur society. Their arts tend to be more action-packed compared to those of other species, reflecting their dynamic and vigorous culture. Epic poems, battle chants, and dramatic reenactments of famous conflicts are common forms of artistic expression.

Interaction with Other Species

Brannur are respected for their combat skills and strategic minds. They often serve as mercenaries, bodyguards, or military advisors, forming alliances with other species based on mutual respect and shared goals. After the breach Brannur can now be found in small communities all over Kastorix with no nation with a Brannur majority.

Military and Defense

Brannur are known to be great fighters and strategists, and many brannur serve in armies and mercenary companies across the world. Their military tactics are rooted in ancient traditions, blending discipline with adaptability. They excel in both offensive and defensive warfare, often employing phalanx formations and coordinated strikes to overwhelm their enemies. Brannur warriors are trained to fight in various terrains and conditions, making them versatile and reliable in battle.

Spear's Legacy

One of the most notable aspects of brannur culture is the Spears Legacy, a world-renowned mercenary group with ancient techniques and rites dating back to before The Draconium. Many brannur dedicate their lives to joining this prestigious company and walking its ancient halls. The fortress of Spear's Legacy, filled with invaluable secrets and knowledge, fell during The Breach. Only the Delta Brigade remains active, leading some to find it suspicious how quickly the fortress fell despite its formidable defenses and history of resilience.

Magic

While not inherently magical, some brannur practice battle magic, using enchanted weapons and armor to enhance their combat abilities. Their magic is often focused on strength, endurance, and tactical advantage, reflecting their pragmatic and martial culture. Rituals to bless weapons and invoke the favor of their warrior deities are common before major battles.
