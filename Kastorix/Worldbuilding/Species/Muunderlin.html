Muunderlin

Muunderlin are strange creatures with a hippo-like lower body and a sturdy humanoid upper body. They live in a specific region of western Tharn in cooperation with commune there. Muunderlin are sapients, though rarely present on the world stage. They prefer to never leave their sacred groves and their natural strength and connection to water magic means few things can make them.

Physical Characteristics

Muunderlin have sturdy bodies, standing over 2 meters tall and 3-4.5 meters long. They have tough, gray skin of various hues. Their faces are humanoid but a large snout and tusks. Their arms are bulky and short, and they have a short tail.
Muunderlin are omnivores,. living of plants and hunting and fishing.
