Shyvarjarna

Shyvarjarna are species of intelligent large spider-like creatures that live in sedentary colonies, in The Caverns Below. Shyvarjarna can be over 3 meters tall and are physically imposing, however their inability to cast spells limit their power.

Shyvarjarna colonies, called webs, are very long lasting, with some being present for over a thousand years. Shyvarjarna rarely leave the territory of their webs, though some of them welcome outsiders to come and train. Their are known for their skills in alchemy, their web cities and their unique martial arts.

Physical Characteristics

Shyvarjarna are the third largest sapient species after dragons and muunderlin. While they resemble spiders at a glance, they heads and front legs are proportionally larger. They are dexterous and agile, and wield custom made tools with their forelegs. They can also spin a strong type of silk that they use in construction and crafting.

Diet

Shyvarjarna are omnivores. They tend to construct large mushroom plantations along which they practice along with the herding of cave oxen. Some webs are skilled hunters, stalking the caverns for food.

Society and Culture

Community Structure

Shyvarjarna webs are typically lead by a council of elders, though some have one sole ruler instead. Webs tend to hold a few hundreds members each, tough the larger ones might have thousands.

Settlements

Shyvarjarna webs are intricate and expansive, serving as both homes and fortresses. They are designed to withstand the test of time and are constantly maintained and expanded. Each web has unique traditions, often revolving around the maintenance and defence of the web, as well as the veneration of ancient webs. Difference in beliefs between webs can sometimes create long standing feuds that can last for hundreds of years.

Economy and Craftsmanship

Shyvarjarna are renowned for their alchemical prowess. They create potions, elixirs, and other concoctions that are highly sought after by other species. Their webs are not just homes but works of art, with complex patterns and structures that serve both practical and aesthetic purposes.

Shyvarjarna craft tools tailored to their unique physiology, enhancing their dexterity and strength. These tools are often made from a using chitin and silk, alongside metal and other materials found in the caverns. Shyvarjarna sculptures are valued highly by some collectors, due their skill and uniqueness.

Religion and Beliefs

Shyvarjarna hold their ancestors in high regard, believing that their spirits guide and protect the web. Ancient webs are considered sacred sites. Many shyvarjarna believe in the interconnectedness of all living things, The Web of The World, a grand metaphysical web that connects to every living creature across Kastorix.

Daily Life and Practices

Education

Young Shyvarjarna learn through apprenticeships, shadowing elders to master various skills, from alchemy to web maintenance. Martial arts are a core part of their education, focusing on agility, strength, and strategy. This training is not just for defence but also for personal discipline and growth.

Art and Music

Shyvarjarna create intricate patterns in their webs that tell stories and record history. These patterns are a form of art that is both visual and tactile. Some webs trade in dyes to color and decorate their web arts. Shyvarjarna also produce music using their webs, creating vibrations that resonate through the caverns. This music is a form of communication and expression, often used in rituals and celebrations.

Interaction with Other Species

Shyvarjarna engage in trade with other species, exchanging their alchemical products and silk for goods they cannot produce themselves. Some webs form alliances with other species, offering protection and knowledge in exchange for resources and support. Despite their preference for isolation, Shyvarjarna are not averse to conflict when their webs are threatened. Their martial skills and strategic minds make them formidable opponents.

Military and Defense

Shyvarjarna webs are designed to be defensible, with traps and barriers woven into the structure. The silk's strength and elasticity make it an effective barrier against intruders. Their unique martial arts style leverages their agility and strength, focusing on disabling opponents rather than killing them. In times of conflict, Shyvarjarna use their alchemical knowledge to create weapons and defenses, from explosive concoctions to debilitating poisons.

Magic

Unlike most sapients shyvarjarna cannot cast spells, due to their small mana wells. This prevents them from practicing magic and limits their power. The exact reason for their small mana wells is unknown and a mystery to scholars, both shyvarjarna and outsiders. Some shyvarjarna believe their inability of casting magic is due to a curse placed on them or a limitation that could be surpassed with enough power or an esoteric rite, but no known cases of spellcasting shyvarjarna have been found.

While they are incapable of casting spells, shyvarjarna are skilled alchemists and have their own form of artifice that weaves runes on their webs.
