Coatl

Coatls are rare magical creatures that inhabit jungles and forests. Resembling large serpents with shimmering feathered wings, they are renowned for their formidable magical power and profound intelligence. Revered as nature spirits in many cultures across Kastorix, coatls are seen as wise and just guardians of their domains. Although they cannot speak or understand languages in the traditional sense, they can, through an unknown magic, sense and transmit emotions and memories to nearby creatures. This form of communication often manifests as abstract images and vague proverbs, adding to their mystique.

Physical Characteristics

Coatls are vibrant and colorful, with scales and feathers in hues of yellow, orange, and red on their front, while their backs often have a striking cyan or dark blue. Their birdlike wings, though not required for flight due to their magical nature, add to their majestic appearance. A few feathers also adorn the end of their tails. Coatls are slimmer than many large snakes, and despite their size, they are not particularly strong physically, relying more on their magical abilities for defense and movement.

Settlements

Due to their highly magical nature, coatls need to reside near mana nodes to thrive. They prefer forests and jungle environments but can occasionally be found in other areas rich in mana. Typically solitary, coatls are nonetheless welcoming to travelers who respect their habitats. They are known to fiercely defend their territories from invaders and hostile species using a combination of magic, trickery, and strategy. Although they do not build structures, coatls often decorate their surroundings with inscribed stones, flower patches, and glowing lights, creating an enchanting environment.

Interaction with Other Species

In many beliefs, especially among naga cultures, coatls are revered as wise nature spirits. They are generally friendly and nurturing, though often mysterious in their ways. The wisdom imparted by coatls can vary, as each one is an individual with unique experiences and perspectives.

Soulbonds

Coatls are capable of forming soulbonds but are highly selective in choosing their partners. They seek individuals who share their values and demonstrate significant magical prowess, as a bonded coatl requires substantial mana if it is to be away from its node. Spellcasters who successfully form a soulbond with a coatl gain access to its powerful magic and profound wisdom, making such a bond immensely valuable and sought after.

Magic

Coatls are among the most magical creatures on Kastorix, intrinsically connected to the aspects of light, air and fire. This connection allows them to float effortlessly and illuminate dark environments. Over time, coatls appear to learn and master additional spells, showcasing their adaptability and intelligence. They are reputed to summon firestorms, rays of light, and hurricane winds to protect themselves and their habitats, further cementing their status as powerful and mystical beings.
