Dragon

Dragons are the largest and most long-lived of the sentient species on Kastorix. Renowned for their immense physical strength and magical prowess, dragons hold a unique and sometimes revered position in the world. They are inherently attuned to one of the aspects of magic, which grants them special abilities and powers linked to their aspect. Most dragons live in The Draconium, the only surviving nation on Avijor.

Physical Characteristics

Dragons are massive creatures, ranging from 4 to 7 meters in length when fully grown. Their scales, which can become as tough as adamantium, provide excellent protection. Dragons possess sharp claws, long tails and large, powerful wings that allow them to fly. Although they rarely permit others to ride them, exceptions exist, particularly within the Draconium's intelligence agency, the Wings of Truth. Since The Breach, dragons have been more willing to carry troops into battle or serve as mobile battle stations for mages and artillery units.

Society and Culture

In ancient times, dragons often feuded and engaged in fierce battles, nearly driving themselves to extinction. To avoid this fate, they founded The Draconium, a nation and sanctuary where almost all dragons now reside. The Draconium is home to almost all the dragons in the world as well as the Dragonkin, a species very much connected to dragons.

Interaction with Other Species

Dragons are rarely seen outside of the Draconium, making them a rare sight in other parts of Kastorix. Notable exceptions include the infamous Stezic the Tyrant and the archmage Chaolix. While they rarely leave the Draconium, dragons still an important role in the political landscape of all of Kastorix

Military and Defense

The physical and magical might of dragons causes them to be powerful threats on the battlefield. Dragons have defeated armies on their own and their overwhelming strength deters would be aggressors.

Magic

All dragons are born with an innate connection to a specific aspect of magic. This attunement allows them to wield dragonfire—a potent breath weapon associated with their aspect. Additionally, dragons can learn to imbue their scales and physical attacks with their elemental magic, further enhancing their combat abilities.

Dragons can practice wizardry, though few of them become skilled at it. Unlike others who practice magic, dragons mana wells, doesn't grow noticeably from practice, making magical studies less worthwhile. The rare dragons that become archmages, like Hezeroth and Chaolix, are however some of the most powerful beings in Kastorix

Even fewer Dragons practice alchemy or artifice, as the most of the equipment to do so is the designed for humanoid creatures.
