Gnomes

Gnomes are a distinctive species of short humanoids, typically resembling shorter humans but often exhibiting a range of unusual hair and eye colors. They are known for their strong connection to magic, which grows stronger as they age. Unlike other mortal species, however, gnomes cannot be born with an attunement and practice sorcery. Instead, as they age, many gnomes will pick up a bit of elemental magic from the area they live in, becoming "pseudo-attuned." Many gnomes practice wizardry, often competing with nagas over mastery of the arcane. Gnomes are also famous for their inventions and contributions to the fields of artifice and alchemy.


Gnomes

Population: 37 Million
Average Lifespan: 150 years
Size: 0.9 - 1.3 meters
Rarity: Common

Physical Characteristics

Gnomes are short, ranging from 0.9 to 1.3 meters in height. They typically resemble shorter humans but often exhibit a range of unusual hair and eye colors, such as bright blue, green, purple, and pink. As they age, their connection to magic intensifies, often reflected in their physical appearance. Older gnomes might have more vivid and striking features due to their accumulated magical energy.

Society and Culture

Community Structure

Gnome society is often notably decentralized, split into city-state with separate governments. This allows gnomes to choose cities based on personal preference, as each city often has its unique character or "gimmick." Power within these city-states is usually held by councils or elected officials, often composed of the most skilled wizards, artificers, and scholars. This meritocratic system encourages innovation and creativity. The famous city states in southern Avijor were organized into a loose federation known as The Silverleaf League

Settlements

Gnome settlements are often bustling hubs of invention and magical experimentation. They are known for their intricate architecture, combining practical design with whimsical elements. Buildings are frequently adorned with mechanical contraptions, enchanted lights, and other magical devices. Gnome city-states along the southern coast of Avijor were renowned for their advanced infrastructure and magical defenses before the Breach.

Economy and Craftsmanship

Gnomes generally look down on manual work and physical labor, preferring to automate as much as possible using magic, golems, or hired labor. They excel in artifice, creating a wide range of magical devices and automata. Gnomes also thrive in creative fields, scholarly pursuits, and administrative roles. Their advanced craftsmanship and innovative spirit have made them key players in trade, particularly in magical items and advanced technology.

Religion and Beliefs

Few gnomes are religious and their cities have few temples and places of worship. Some gnomes believe in, or at least pray to Lady Fortuna, a manifestation of luck itself. Prayers, sincere or not, are sometimes given before important trade deals or new prototype tests.

Daily Life and Practices

Education

Education in gnome society is highly valued, with a strong emphasis on magic, artifice, and innovation. Young gnomes are encouraged to explore their interests and talents, leading to a highly educated and skilled population. Institutions similar to guilds often provide specialized training in various magical and technical disciplines.

Art and Music

Gnome art and music are as innovative as their technology. They often incorporate magical elements into their creations, resulting in dynamic and interactive art pieces and musical performances. Gnome festivals are known for their vibrant displays of light, sound, and magical effects.

Interaction with Other Species

Traditionally, Gnomes lived in city-states along the southern coast of Avijor. This geographical advantage afforded them more time to escape The Breach compared to other species. Tens of thousands of Gnomes were evacuated via airships and ordinary boats. Some city-states managed to hold off the Breach for a while, while others attempted different strategies for survival. The city of Tindwyl, for instance, was teleported in a grand ritual, and other cities mysteriously disappeared.

Even before the Breach, some Gnomes had settled on the southern continent for trade, leading to the establishment of Gnomish enclaves in coastal cities. This Gnomish influx contributed to the formation of new nations, like Straix and Glarnast. Gnomes, like humans, are known to adapt well to other cultures and communities, doing trades and making acquaintances. They also maintain active relationships with species like yirte and Naga, often collaborating on magical and technological projects.

Military and Defense

Gnomes rely heavily on their magical and technical prowess for defense. Their city-states are often protected by intricate magical wards, mechanical defenses, and golem armies. The few gnome soldiers that exist are typically equipped with advanced weaponry and magical devices, making them formidable opponents despite their small stature. They favor strategies that emphasize clever tactics and the use of their inventions over brute force.

Magic

Gnomes are deeply connected to magic, with their abilities growing stronger as they age. Unlike other species, Gnomes cannot be born with an attunement and practice sorcery. Instead, many gnomes become "pseudo-attuned" to elemental magic from their surroundings as they age, giving them a connection similar to an attunement.

Gnomes are known to be skilled at wizardry and especially artifice. They have made a vast array of magical devices and constructs that aid in daily life, research, and defense. Examples include the airship construction yards of Thune, the defensive golems of Tuln, and the floating lanterns of Tindwyl.
