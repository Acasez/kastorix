Heksan

Heksan is a forested nation in northeastern Meibor, known for its rich natural resources, particularly timber and rare herbs, as well as its advanced practices in herbalism and alchemy. The country is a loose confederation of duchies, each ruled by noble houses that have banded together for mutual defense and prosperity. The unique Yirte culture, with its deep connection to the forest and natural world, is important in Heksan, from its architecture to its military strategies. In recent years, Heksan has become a hub for alchemical research and production, exporting potent potions and alchemicals across the continent.

Geography

Heksan borders Theze to the northwest, Ellios to the west, Ganderei to the south and Deshir to the east. Heksan’s geography is defined by its vast and diverse forestlands, which cover much of its 541,000 km² territory. The forests range from dense, temperate woodlands dominated by towering redwoods to the more tropical edge of the Meiboran jungle in the west.
- Forests and Woodlands: The central and southern parts of Heksan are covered in ancient forests, with thick canopies that create a shadowy, serene atmosphere. The trees here are ancient and massive, with some towering over 100 meters tall. The underbrush is rich with diverse plant life, providing the raw materials for Heksan’s renowned herbal medicines.
- Northern Coastline: Heksan’s northern coastline stretches along the Sea of Shards, with rugged cliffs and sandy beaches. The coastal towns are vital centers of trade, where ships from neighboring nations bring goods and news. The sea is dotted with small islands, many of which are uninhabited but are home to rare herbs and other natural resources. The most notable island is the Island of Shades, a mysterious and largely unexplored place known for its ever-present shadows and the fortified town of Shadewatch.
- Mountains and Highlands: To the east, near the border with Deshir, the land rises into rugged highlands and low mountains. These regions are less densely populated, but they hold rich mineral deposits and rare alchemical ingredients. The terrain here is harsher, with colder temperatures and starker landscapes.
- Rivers and Lakes: Heksan is crisscrossed by a network of rivers and lakes, providing fresh water and transportation routes. The largest lake Glademeet lies on its southern border and is the second largest lake in Meibor.

Government

Heksan’s government is an elective monarchy, unique in its decentralized structure. The nation is divided into seven duchies, each with its own ruling noble house. These duchies operate semi-independently, with significant autonomy in managing their internal affairs.
- The Council of Dukes: The highest governing body in Heksan is the Council of Dukes, composed of the leaders of the seven duchies. Every six years, the council elects one of its members to serve as the Forest Warden, a position akin to a monarch or head of state. The Forest Warden is responsible for overseeing Heksan’s foreign relations, coordinating defense, and managing issues that affect the entire nation. The role is prestigious but comes with the challenge of balancing the interests of the duchies.
- Duchies and Nobles: Each duchy is ruled by a duke or duchess, who governs with the support of a council of advisors, typically composed of other nobles, influential merchants, and respected alchemists. These councils are responsible for local governance, including law enforcement, trade, and resource management. The duchies have strong traditions of independence and often have distinct cultural practices and local laws.
- Laws and Traditions: Despite the autonomy of the duchies, certain laws and traditions are universally respected across Heksan. These include the protection of the forests, the regulation of alchemical practices, and the maintenance of roads and bridges that connect the duchies. The yirte people have a strong oral tradition, and many of their laws are based on ancient customs passed down through generations.

History

Heksan was founded during the Thezan wars of expansions, when the seven duchies composing felt threatened by the quickly growing nation.

Economy

Heksan’s expertise in herbalism and alchemy has made it a center of innovation and production in Meibor. The nation’s forests are home to an extraordinary variety of plants, many of which are found nowhere else in the world. Plants such as Silverleaf, Nightshade Blossom and Spirit Lilies are exported and used in alchemy. Heksan’s alchemists are particularly known for their extremely valuable potions of regeneration, which can heal even the most grievous wounds. Heksan also has the Girith school of alchemy, the worlds premier school in alchemy, which takes in students from across the continent.

Heksan also sells large amounts of timber from its forests. It imports metals, artifice and other goods.

Society and Culture

The culture of Heksan is deeply intertwined with the natural world, particularly the forests that cover much of the nation. The yirte, the dominant species, have a profound respect for nature and a deep understanding of its cycles and secrets.
- Connection to Nature: Heksan cities and towns are often built in harmony with the forest, rather than in opposition to it. Buildings are constructed from locally sourced timber and are often designed to blend into the surroundings, with homes built around or even within the trees. The yirte are known for their ability to traverse the forests with ease, using hidden paths, bridges, and tunnels that outsiders rarely notice.
- Herbalism and Alchemy: Herbalism is a revered tradition in Heksan, with many families passing down secret recipes and techniques through generations. Alchemy has become an increasingly important part of Heksan’s identity, with numerous laboratories and schools dedicated to the study and production of potions, elixirs, and alchemical compounds. Heksan’s alchemists are respected and sometimes feared for their knowledge of both healing and destructive brews.
- Festivals and Rites: The people of Heksan celebrate numerous festivals tied to the cycles of nature, such as the Festival of the First Bloom, which marks the beginning of spring, and the Night of Shadows, a midsummer celebration that honors the spirits of the forest. Rituals often involve offerings of herbs, flowers, and food to the spirits and the forest itself, asking for protection and bounty.
- Art and Craftsmanship: Heksan’s artisans are known for their intricate wood carvings, herbal tinctures, and alchemical creations. Their crafts are often imbued with symbolic meanings and are used both in everyday life and in rituals. Alchemical knowledge is considered both a science and an art, with master alchemists enjoying high status in society.

Military

While Heksan lacks a formal national military, its defense is ensured by the well-trained and highly specialized forces maintained by the individual duchies. The country’s defensive strategies are deeply rooted in its geography and the expertise of its people.
- Duchy Guards: Each duchy maintains its own guard forces, responsible for local defense and law enforcement. These forces are trained in guerilla warfare, using the forests to their advantage. The Yirte warriors are experts in hit-and-run tactics, ambushes, and the use of the terrain to outmaneuver and outlast their enemies.
- Forest Warfare: Heksan’s forests are a natural fortress, with retractable bridges, hidden paths, and concealed fortifications. During the war with Theze 60 years ago, Heksan’s forces used these tactics to devastating effect, repelling a much larger army by drawing them into the forests and using the terrain to their advantage.
- Alchemical Warfare: The duchy guards are supported by alchemists who provide them with potions and elixirs to enhance their combat abilities or hinder their enemies. Heksan sharpshooters use small, enchanted bows with poison tipped arrows to assassinate enemy commanders and battlemages.
- Coastal Defense: The northern coast is guarded by a series of fortresses and watchtowers, with ships patrolling the waters to deter invasions.
