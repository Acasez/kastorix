Renue

Renue is a small yet influential republic located on the northwestern coast of Meibor. Known for its proximity to and deep connection with Ishyin Academy, the preeminent magical institution in the world, Renue enjoys a reputation as a hub of magical innovation and scholarly pursuits. Its strong agricultural base complements its intellectual prowess, making it a vital player in the region despite its modest size.

Renue’s population is diverse, with a notable presence of nagas and gnomes, contributing to its rich cultural tapestry and innovative spirit. The nation is renowned for its peaceful coexistence of academic brilliance and agricultural abundance. The five families of Renue are a group influential families with large amount of political power in Renue.

Geography

Renue is located on the Renuanan Peninsula bordering Ishyin to the north, Tindwyl to west on the tip of peninsula, Theze to the east and Aldarna to the southeast. On its southern border lies the Diavar inlet and across it the republic of Thune. The land its located on is a flat plain with occasional rolling hills, covered in farmlands.

Government

Renue is a parliamentary republic, holding elections every four years. Its governance is characterized by two dominant factions:
- The Academics: Strongly aligned with Ishyin Academy, they advocate for policies that enhance Renue’s magical reputation and technological advancements.
- The Farmers: Representing the agricultural backbone of the nation, they prioritize rural development and food security.
While officially independent, Renue’s policies often align closely with Ishyin Academy’s interests, leading some to accuse it of being a puppet state for the academy. However, this relationship has allowed Renue to thrive both magically and economically.

History

Renue has existed for centuries, originally founded by a coalition of farmers and merchants who sought to create a peaceful and prosperous community. Its strategic location made it a natural trading hub and an ally to Ishyin Academy as the latter grew in prominence.
The magical disaster known as The Breach cemented Ishyin Academy as the foremost magical center in the world. In response, Renue deepened its ties with the academy, aligning its policies and infrastructure to support and benefit from Ishyin’s dominance.
Renue’s commitment to magic has allowed it to flourish even as regional tensions have grown, particularly with neighboring Aldarna.

Economy

Renue has many fertile farmlands and exports food across Meibor.
Many mages settle in Renue after their stay at the academy and and the nation enjoys many magical services and new artifice and magical products.

Society and Culture

Renue's culture is highly magical with one the highest rate of adepts per capita in the world.

Military

Renue has a military structured inspired by Jadiar, with battlemages as its primary force, guarded by heavily armored soldiers. Recent ideological conflicts with Aldarna has made the nation expand its forces, recruiting many Ishyin graduates to serve as battlemages.
Renue also has a small airship fleet.
