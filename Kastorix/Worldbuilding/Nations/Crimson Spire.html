Crimson Spire

The federation of Glarnast is one of the largest post-breach nations on Kastorix, emerging from the unification of individual states on the eastern coast of Santsim. This unification was driven by the need to address the shifting politics and threats in the post-breach world. Governed by a council of representatives elected every three years, Glarnast is known for its diversity and cosmopolitan nature.

Geography

Glarnast lies on the eastern coast of Santsim, on fertile fields and forested hillsides. At 337 000km² it's the second largest nation by size on Santsim, third if you count Quarthax as one country, much of it's territory is coastal and it shares a maritime border with Jadiar across the Jasman strait. To the northwest Glarnast borders Tharn and reaches into part of the Tharn Mangrove Forest, to the west lies the kingdom of Qual, a border which is currently quite fortified due to current tensions between. To the southwest is the kingdom of Odiana.
Glarnast is quite warm, though not as hot as Jadiar across the strait. The northern parts has a subtropical climate, with the more southern regions becoming more temperate. Cool winds coming from colder sea regions from below the strait help lower temperatures.

Government

Glarnast operates as a democratic federation with a decentralized structure. While the federal government manages the united military and enforces laws related to democracy and voting, individual states retain autonomy over many policies. This decentralization leads to frequent and intense debates on governance. Glarnast is composed of five states

  • Abaran.

  • Narnem.

History

Prior to unification, the republics and kingdoms on the eastern shores of Santsim had a history of trade and cooperation, often banding together against threats from nations like Qual and Odiana. The influx of refugees from Avijor, fleeing the breach, brought new inventions, advancements in artifice, and democratic ideals, significantly influencing the political landscape.
Glarnast was founded with three states and expanded rapidly, incorporating two more within five years. The northern kingdom of Rajven saw its king abdicate due to internal and external pressures, while Narnem experienced a revolution against its ruler's attempts to cling to power.
This rapid expansion has led to regional tensions, with nearby nations forming alliances against Glarnast. Southern kingdoms accuse it of spreading propaganda to fuel its growth, while Qual has bolstered its military, positioning itself as a defender of traditional ways.

Economy

Initially partially industrialized, the states within Glarnast have seen significant investment in further industrialization since unifying. The nation is renowned for producing a wide array of manufactured goods, both magical and mundane. Key industries include the Runegun industry and numerous spellforges. Additionally, Glarnast's fertile lands make it a major agricultural producer, contributing substantially to its economy.

Society and Culture

Glarnast boasts a diverse and cosmopolitan society with a rich tapestry of species and cultures. While some cultural blending occurs, the states, especially former kingdoms, maintain distinct identities. As a major center of commerce and trade, Glarnast connects the continents of Santsim and Meibor, fostering a wealthy merchant class with significant political influence. To counterbalance this, several labor organizations and unions have emerged.

Military

Glarnast's military is sizeable, though it lacks specialization. The army includes cavalry regiments and elite vanguards from its founding nations, but the bulk of the force consists of regular infantry. The nation’s proficiency in manufacturing runeguns is notable, with its spellforges producing high-quality models.
Additionally, Glarnast fields a considerable number of golems, intended to serve as the vanguard of its army. While it maintains a small Airships fleet, proposals to fund a dreadnought flagship have been rejected due to financial constraints.
