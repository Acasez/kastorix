Levia

Levia is a mountainous nation situated on the western edge of the Everglow Mountains, bordering the arid lands of Jadiar. Known for its wealth of rare ores and gemstones, Levia has a long history as a center of mining and metallurgy. The nation is a mercantile republic, where political power is largely in the hands of wealthy mine owners. While Levia's economy has seen significant growth with the advent of airship ports, enabling broader trade, it is also grappling with increasing inequality and corruption, leading to social unrest and the rise of revolutionary movements.

Geography

Levia is defined by its rugged terrain, with nearly half of the country situated above 1,000 meters in elevation. The landscape is dominated by the towering peaks of the Everglow Mountains, which form a natural barrier on its eastern border. The country's western edge transitions into the arid, rocky lands that border the expansive Jadieran Desert.
- Everglow Mountains: These mountains are the lifeblood of Levia, rich in mineral deposits and home to the nation’s extensive mining operations. The mountains are steep, with jagged cliffs and narrow valleys. The region is largely arid, but cooler than the adjacent desert, with sparse vegetation adapted to the harsh conditions.
- Subterranean Settlements: Over centuries, the Levian people have carved out a network of tunnels and mines within the mountains, some of which have developed into full-fledged subterranean settlements. These underground towns are lit by bioluminescent crystals and fed by mushroom farms, creating a unique environment where miners and their families live and work.
- Cave Systems and The Caverns Below: Some of Levia’s tunnels connect to vast cave systems, including portions of The Caverns Below. These dark, sprawling networks are both a source of additional mineral wealth and a constant threat, as they are home to dangerous creatures. These tunnels also meet with scouts from the Emerald Empire .

Government

Levia is a presidential republic, one of the oldest on the continent of Meibor. The president, elected every four years, serves as both the head of state and government. Despite its republican ideals, the political landscape of Levia is heavily influenced by wealthy mine owners and industrialists who hold significant sway over the legislative and executive branches.
- Political Corruption and Social Unrest: In recent decades, the government has been criticized for its increasing subservience to the interests of the mining elite. This has led to growing economic inequality and discontent among the general population. A revolutionary movement has begun to gain traction, calling for the restoration of true democratic principles and greater equity in wealth distribution.
- Local Governance: While the central government is based in the capital city of Runehold, local governance in Levia is often handled by regional councils, many of which are dominated by the interests of mining companies. These councils manage day-to-day affairs, including the maintenance of infrastructure and regulation of mining activities.

History

Levia has a history that stretches back over a thousand years. The region was originally settled by various clans who were drawn to the rich mineral deposits in the Everglow Mountains. These clans eventually unified into a kingdom, which became one of the early regional powers in Meibor.
- Golem Crafting: The Levian Kingdom was renowned for its golem crafting, with some of the most advanced and powerful stone golems of the time. These golems were used for both defense and labor, and their designs have influenced Levian golem-making to this day.
532 years ago, the kingdom was overthrown in a popular uprising against the tyrannical rule of King Srarr, whose oppressive policies had driven the people to revolt. The monarchy was abolished, and the Levian Republic was established as one of the earliest democracies in Meibor.
- Early Republic: The early years of the republic were marked by a strong commitment to democratic ideals, with power being more evenly distributed among the citizens. However, over time, the influence of wealthy mining interests grew, leading to the gradual erosion of these ideals.

Economy

Levia's economy is heavily dependent on mining and metallurgy, with its wealth derived from the extraction and processing of rare ores and gemstones. The nation is one of the leading exporters of precious metals and stones in Meibor.
- Key Exports:
- Adamantium: Known for its unmatched strength and durability, adamantium from Levia is highly sought after for weapons, armor, and industrial uses.
- Sparkgold: A rare and magical metal known for its connection to aspect of fire and use in many alloys.
- Gemstones and Crystals: Levia exports a wide variety of gemstones, including sapphires, emeralds, and rubies, as well as magical crystals used in various arcane applications.
- Airship Trade: The development of airship ports in Levia has revolutionized its economy, allowing it to bypass the dangerous overland routes through the Jadieran Desert. This has opened up new markets and increased the speed and volume of trade, bringing greater wealth to the nation but also exacerbating economic disparities.
- Imports: Despite its mineral wealth, Levia lacks arable land and relies heavily on imports of food and other agricultural products. The nation also imports advanced artifice and technology to enhance its mining operations and golem manufacturing.

Society and Culture

Levia’s culture is shaped by the harsh, mountainous environment, fostering a spirit of resilience and ingenuity among its people.
- Craftsmanship and Innovation: Levian culture places a high value on craftsmanship, particularly in metalwork and gem cutting. The nation’s artisans are among the best in Meibor, producing intricate jewelry, enchanted artifacts, and finely wrought weapons and armor.
- Underground Life: Some Levian citizens live in subterranean settlements, where they have developed a unique way of life. These underground communities are close-knit, with a strong sense of camaraderie among miners and their families. Festivals and rituals often revolve around the earth and stone, reflecting the people’s deep connection to the land.

Military

Levia's military is small but well-equipped, designed to defend the nation’s mountainous borders and protect its valuable mines from both external and internal threats.
- Adamantium-Clad Soldiers: The Levian military is known for its elite soldiers clad in adamantium armor, making them exceptionally tough and resistant to conventional attacks. These soldiers are often equipped with adamantium weapons, making them formidable in close combat.
- Golem: Levia’s military includes a specialized division of golems, building on the ancient traditions of the Levian Kingdom. These golems are used primarily for defense, patrolling the borders, and guarding key infrastructure such as mines and airship ports. The golems are crafted from durable stone and are enhanced with modern enchantments to increase their combat effectiveness.
- Gem Brigades: Some of the wealthier mining companies maintain private militias, known as gem brigades, to protect their interests. These brigades are well-trained and often better equipped than the regular military, leading to concerns about their influence and loyalty in times of conflict.
