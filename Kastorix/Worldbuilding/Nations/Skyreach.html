Skyreach

Skyreach is the largest yirte community in Kastorix, located at the base of the Everglow Mountains. The city is built around a colossal Giasantra tree, from which it got its name, which soars five hundred meters into the air and is visible for miles. The capital city is integrated into this massive tree and its neighbors, forming a network of treehouses and ropeways. Other cities within Skyreach are similarly constructed amid treetops and mountain cliffs. Skyreach is renowned for its griffin cavalry, the world's largest flying cavalry unit.

Geography

Skyreach is located in the dense temperate forest that surrounds the colossal tree at it's center, where it's capital lies. The forest has many giasantra trees that regularly reaches over 200 meters, but the one it's capital is based around is far taller than any others. Skyreach's capital is built into the the tree via countless bridges and treehouses.

While the capital has a plurality of its inhabitants, Skyreach has many other villages and cities in the forests built in traditional ways over generations.

Government

Skyreach is governed by a council of elders who prioritize tradition, harmony with nature, and self-sufficiency. This council, while powerful and influential, is often slow to adopt new ideas and changes, preferring to maintain the status quo and uphold ancient customs.

History

Skyreach is as ancient as the tree it is named after, which has stood for at least a thousand years. Over time, the community has expanded, absorbing and settling nearby areas. The stability and continuity of Skyreach have made it a bastion of yirte culture and tradition.

In recent years, however, many young yirte have left Skyreach in search of opportunities elsewhere, driven by the overcrowding of the tree cities and the increasing strain on the plantations to feed the growing population.

Economy

Due to its isolationist policies and the values of its elder council, Skyreach rarely engages in trade with the outside world. The community is largely self-sufficient, with much of its food production coming from carefully tended tree plantations and traditional hunting practices. Skyreach is known for its skilled craftspeople and the cultivation of rare plants, including a few silverwood groves, although these goods are seldom exported.

Society and Culture

Skyreach society is deeply rooted in tradition and a strong connection to nature. The yirte of Skyreach live in harmony with their environment, using sustainable practices to ensure the longevity of their home. The architecture of Skyreach reflects this ethos, with cities seamlessly integrated into the natural landscape of treetops and cliffs.

Cultural life in Skyreach revolves around communal activities, ceremonies, and the wisdom of the elders. The community places a high value on storytelling, music, and dance, which are integral to preserving their heritage and passing it down through generations.

Military

Skyreach's military strength lies in its griffin riders, an elite force of aerial warriors who fight with bows and spells from atop trained griffins. These griffin riders are highly skilled and have protected Skyreach and its neighboring communities for at least three hundred years. Their unparalleled aerial combat capabilities make them a formidable force, rarely matched by any enemy.

Skyreach has little infantry, relying instead on its natural defenses and isolation to deter ground based threats
