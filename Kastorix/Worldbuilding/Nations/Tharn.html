Tharn

Tharn is a unique nation nestled in the vast mangrove forests of northern Santsim. Its druidic commune governance and focus on environmental harmony set it apart from other nations on Kastorix. Tharn's isolationist stance has allowed it to develop a distinct culture and economy, centered around the sustainable use of its rich biosphere.

Geography

Most of Tharn is hot and humid, covered by mangrove forest with a rich biodiversity. It lies west of El-Shavri, southeast of Qual and east of Glarnast. To the south lies the Ashen Lands, a sparsely populated hot area with much volcanic activity. Tharn has no formal capital but it's largest city of Avirest serves as the closest equivalence.

Government

Tharn is governed by a decentralized druidic commune, where decisions are made collectively by local councils and communities. This structure promotes a deep connection to the land and its resources. The community appoints Swampwalkers, skilled individuals who act as rangers, mages, and messengers, connect the various towns and villages.

History

The area around Tharn has been inhabited for over a thousand years, with early settlers drawn to the rich biodiversity of the mangrove forests. The nation of Tharn was formally established 411 years ago, in 963, as a response to growing external threats and the need for a unified defense of the region's unique ecosystem.

Tharn chose to remain isolated from global conflicts, focusing on internal development and environmental preservation. This policy has allowed it to maintain its unique culture and avoid the destructive impacts of war. Tharn's natural defenses, combined with its skilled mages and rangers, have deterred potential invaders. The nation's landscape and wildlife are formidable obstacles for any would-be conqueror.

Economy

Tharn exports many rare ingredients and herbs found in its unique biome, which are highly sought after for alchemical and medicinal purposes. Due to the challenges of mining in the wet terrain, metal is imported to meet industrial needs. The nation has a strong tradition of magical teaching, with many skilled mages contributing to both the economy and defense.

Society and Culture

Tharn has a communal culture with a difference in focus from nearby nations. It's decentralized nature leads some to not even consider it a nation, though multiple would be invaders have found that it can defend itself.

Military

Tharn has a small formal military but relies heavily on its natural defenses and the skills of its mages. The mangrove forests and dangerous wildlife make the terrain difficult to navigate for outsiders, providing a significant defensive advantage. Guerrilla tactics and the strategic use of magic further enhance Tharn's ability to protect itself from external threats. Due to its isolation, the number and strength of its mages is unknown to much of the rest of the world.
