Te-Ohuvi

Te-Ohuvi is an archipelago nation located in the Grand Sea, composed of the two large islands of Paratu and Neuvi, along with numerous smaller isles. This kingdom is known for its formidable waverider aquatic cavalry, which uses aquamarine ray mounts. Te-Ohuvi is unique in that it is the only nation with a majority troll population, and its ruler, often referred to simply as the Troll King, governs over a diverse and ancient society.

Geography

Te-Ohuvi is situated in the Grand Sea, northwest of Santsim and southwest of Avijor. The kingdom is centered on two primary islands: Paratu and Neuvi.
- Paratu is the larger of the two, spanning approximately 155,000 km². It is home to the capital city, Telavaste, which is renowned for its impressive coastal fortifications, vibrant marketplaces, and the grand palace of the Troll King. The island features dense tropical forests, rolling hills, and a series of inland lagoons that are vital for the local fishing industry.
- Neuvi, slightly smaller at around 140,000 km², lies southeast of Paratu. This island is more mountainous, dominated by the imposing Hea'ran volcano. Neuvi's rugged terrain and fertile volcanic soil have fostered a unique ecosystem, including rare flora used in traditional troll medicine. The island’s volcanic activity also contributes to the rich mineral deposits found in the surrounding waters, which the trolls have long exploited for crafting.

Beyond these major islands, Te-Ohuvi includes hundreds of smaller isles, some of which are inhabited. Karanora, a small island north of Neuvi, is infamous as the retirement home and laboratory of the paranoid archmage Khizivim. The island’s treacherous wards and traps continue to challenge adventurers seeking the secrets hidden within.

Far to the west across the Grand Sea lie The Shattered Isles, though few travel across the vast sea.

Government

Te-Ohuvi is governed by a decentralized monarchy, where the Troll King reigns as a symbolic and unifying figurehead. The islands and provinces are largely autonomous, with elected governors managing local affairs. These governors, while independent, swear allegiance to the Troll King and uphold the traditions of the kingdom.

The Troll King, though theoretically powerful, prefers to intervene only in matters of great importance or during times of crisis. His role is more that of a cultural and spiritual leader, revered by the people for his wisdom and restraint. The Troll King’s court in Telavaste is a gathering place for leaders from across the archipelago, where matters of trade, defense, and law are discussed in a spirit of cooperation.

History

Early History

Te-Ohuvi’s history is ancient, with the trolls having settled the islands millennia ago. Early troll communities were small and scattered, often engaging in trade and occasional conflict with neighboring islands and mainland cultures. Over time, these communities unified under a single ruler, forming the kingdom of Te-Ohuvi. The kingdom’s location in the Grand Sea made it a crossroads of maritime trade routes, bringing wealth and cultural exchange.

The Wave Wars

From 432 to 444 the nation of El-Shavri attempted to take over the islands creating a series of wars known as the Wave Wars. The wars ended when legendary troll admiral Rajavir sunk the Shavrian fleet using a combination of tactics and water magic.

The Breach

While not directly affected by The Breach, Te-Ohuvi took in some refugees fleeing from the southern city states. While many of them continued traveled onwards to nations like Tharn and Glarnast, the nation now has a significant brannur minority from those that choose to remain.

Economy

While not a trading hub, Te-Ohuvi has long traded with multiple continents, engaging in commerce with both Avijor and Santsim. The kingdom’s economy is driven by its skilled craftsmanship and abundant marine resources.
- Fish and Ocean Goods: The surrounding seas are rich with marine life, and fishing is a vital industry. The trolls also harvest unique ocean goods, including rare shells, pearls, and aquatic plants with medicinal properties.
- Crafted Goods: Traditional troll craftsmanship is highly valued, with exports including intricate wood carvings, woven textiles, and finely crafted tools and weapons. These items are often imbued with minor enchantments, a testament to the trolls’ deep connection with magic.
However, the islands lack significant mineral resources, leading Te-Ohuvi to import metals and artifice from other nations. The trolls have developed a reputation for being skilled traders, leveraging their unique goods to acquire the materials they need.

Society and Culture

Te-Ohuvi’s culture is a rich blend of troll traditions and influences from the diverse peoples who have settled on its shores. The trolls maintain their connection to the natural world, living in harmony with the land and sea. Their art, music, and festivals reflect this deep respect for nature and the spirits they believe inhabit it.
- Troll Crafts: The trolls are known for their craftsmanship, particularly in wood, stone, and shell. Their creations are not only functional but also deeply symbolic, often depicting scenes from troll mythology and history.
- Music and Festivals: Music is an integral part of troll culture, with drums, horns, and flutes playing a central role in their celebrations. The annual Festival of the Sea is a major event, where the trolls honor the spirits of the ocean and give thanks for the bounty it provides.
- Religion and Beliefs: Trolls in Te-Ohuvi follow a form of animism, believing that spirits inhabit the natural world. Shamans play a key role in society, acting as intermediaries between the people and the spirits, and performing rituals to ensure the community’s prosperity and protection.

Military

Te-Ohuvi’s military is small but highly specialized, reflecting the kingdom’s island geography and need for maritime defense.
- Waveriders: The most famous component of Te-Ohuvi’s military is its waveriders, an elite aquatic cavalry. These warriors ride aquamarine rays, using their mounts’ natural abilities to patrol the seas and defend the kingdom’s waters. Waveriders are trained in both traditional combat and water magic, making them formidable opponents in their element.
- Navy: While the kingdom’s navy is small in size, it is well-equipped and primarily tasked with defending the archipelago from pirates and hostile forces. The navy works closely with the waveriders, providing support and transportation for their operations.
Despite its small military, Te-Ohuvi’s strategic location and the strength of its waveriders make it a respected power in the region.
