Narrei

Narrei is an ancient nation situated on the western shore of Lake Yatinue, deep within the heart of the Najanova Jungle. Known for its serene floating gardens and deeply rooted yirte traditions, Narrei stands as a testament to harmony between civilization and nature. Its capital, Shizei-Su, is a picturesque city of waterways and treetop architecture, embodying the yirte philosophy of living in balance with the land.

Despite its small size and isolation, Narrei has maintained its independence for centuries, quietly thriving amidst the challenges of the jungle.

Geography

Narrei’s geography is shaped by its unique location at the northern edge of the Everglow Mountains and along the shore of Lake Yatinue.
- Capital of Shizei-Su: Nestled on the lake’s edge, Shizei-Su is famed for its floating gardens, interconnected walkways, and tree-dwelling neighborhoods that blend seamlessly into the jungle canopy.
- Forested Heartland: Beyond the capital, Narrei is blanketed by dense jungle. Its villages are small, scattered, and built in traditional yirte styles, often integrated into treetops, cliffsides, or hidden within the undergrowth.
- Isolation: Narrei is uniquely positioned with no direct borders with other nations, accessible only by water or perilous jungle paths. This isolation has preserved its traditions and protected it from outside influence.
The surrounding Everglow Mountains bring unique challenges and resources, with mineral-rich slopes and the occasional wyvern presence, while Lake Yatinue provides fish and a vital trade route with Ellios.

Government

Narrei’s government is a council-based democracy, deeply rooted in yirte traditions of communal decision-making.
- Council Composition: Each village elects a representative, while the capital Shizei-Su appoints five council members due to its size and importance.
- Deliberative Politics: Decisions are made slowly and methodically, reflecting the yirte value of caution and respect for tradition. Sudden changes are rare, with the council often seeking consensus before implementing new policies.
- Civic Unity: The council places significant emphasis on maintaining harmony between villages, ensuring that policies align with the cultural values and ecological balance cherished by the people.

History

Narrei’s origins trace back over a thousand years, when the yirte first established settlements along Lake Yatinue. These early communities thrived by living in harmony with the jungle, building homes in trees and fishing the lake’s abundant waters.

Centuries ago, a coalition of villages united to form Narrei as a defensive measure against threats from the Everglow Mountains and roaming jungle predators. This unity evolved into the council system that governs the nation today.

Narrei has maintained its independence despite occasional tensions with neighboring nations, largely due to its remote location and the natural barriers provided by the jungle and mountains.

Economy

Narrei’s economy is modest and relies on sustainable practices, mirroring the yirte’s respect for nature. It exports exotic herbal ingredients, fruits, game and other materials grown and harvested in the jungle. While many goods are locally produced, Narrei imports metal tools and fine fabrics.

Most of the trade is done via Ellios, shipping goods across Lake Yatinue. Trade is tightly regulated; foreign vessels must seek prior clearance to dock in Shizei-Su, reflecting Narrei’s cautious approach to external interaction.

Society and Culture

Narrei’s society revolves around the yirte’s deep spiritual and ecological traditions.
- Harmony with Nature: The yirte’s way of life prioritizes balance and sustainability, reflected in their architecture, agriculture, and hunting practices.
- Spiritual Practices: Rituals honoring the jungle, lake, and ancestors are central to daily life. Ceremonies often take place in treetop temples or floating platforms on the lake.
- Cultural Isolation: While Narrei welcomes trade, it remains culturally insular, valuing its traditions and resisting external influences.
- Art and Craftsmanship: Narrei is known for its intricate woodcarvings, woven textiles, and herbal potions, which combine practical use with artistic beauty.

Festivals celebrating the jungle’s bounty and the changing seasons are vibrant, communal events that reinforce Narrei’s cultural identity.

Military

Narrei’s small military is designed more for defense against natural threats than human conflicts.
- Defensive Focus: Narrei’s isolation and the difficulty of traversing the jungle provide natural protection against armies, reducing the need for a large standing force.
- Jungle Guardians: The military is adept at navigating the dense jungle, using the terrain to their advantage in case of external threats.
- Monster Defense:
- Wyvern Attacks: Patrols near the Everglow Mountains keep watch for wyverns descending to prey on villages.
- Gloom Crocodiles: These dangerous predators lurking near the lake and rivers are another frequent threat.
- Equipment: Soldiers use lightweight, jungle-appropriate gear, including spears, bows, and tools crafted from local materials.

While small, Narrei’s military is well-trained to handle the unique challenges of its environment, ensuring the safety of its people.
