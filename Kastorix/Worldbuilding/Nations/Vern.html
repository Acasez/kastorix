Vern

Vern is an old nation on the eastern and southern coast of Meibor. While technically a republic, it has long been ruled by different wealthy family dynasties that hold considerable power. Vern has lost power and territory during recent years, as the post-breach nations of Straix and Deyton were established along its borders and political unrest threaten the nation.

Geography

Vern's terrain is temperate and fertile, characterized by vast fields and river valleys that support extensive agriculture. It borders Straix and Deyton to the south and Saursi to the north. The geography has shaped Vern's economy and culture, with a strong focus on farming and trade.

Government

Vern is governed as an oligarchic republic, with a Council of Nobles serving as the head of state. The council is composed of representatives from the most influential families, who hold significant political and economic power. The day-to-day administration is overseen by a Prime Minister, who implements policies and manages the bureaucracy. Despite its republican façade, the true power lies with the wealthy dynasties.

History

Vern was founded 873 years ago as a republic, but wealthy families quickly consolidated power. During its golden age, Vern's navy dominated the southern seas, allowing it to control trade routes and exert influence over neighboring nations. This period saw significant economic growth and cultural flourishing.

The rise of airships in shipping and military applications diminished Vern's naval power. Additionally, the establishment of Straix and Deyton along its borders led to territorial losses and political unrest. The wealthy families, while often plotting against each other, unite when faced with external threats to maintain their collective influence.

Economy

Vern's economy is heavily reliant on agriculture, with crops being the primary export. The fertile lands and temperate climate support a variety of crops, making Vern a significant food producer. However, the nation imports metals, textiles, and luxury goods to meet the demands of its wealthy elite and to supplement its industrial needs. Recent investments have been to establish more spellforges and increase the industrial production of the nation.

Society and Culture

Vern's society is marked by the influence of its wealthy dynasties, with a strong emphasis on tradition and lineage. The culture is rich in intrigue and political maneuvering, with spies and covert agents playing significant roles in the power dynamics. Despite these tensions, the people of Vern are united by a shared history and pride in their agricultural heritage.

Military

Vern's military strength has waned in recent years, particularly its once-formidable navy. The growth of airships has rendered its naval power less effective, and political unrest has weakened its overall military capabilities. However, Vern's network of spies and covert agents remains a formidable asset, allowing the nation to gather intelligence and conduct operations behind enemy lines.
