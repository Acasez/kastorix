Theze

The Kingdom of Theze is a relatively young but powerful nation situated on the northern coast of Meibor. Known for its constitutional monarchy, Theze is a dynamic and rapidly growing kingdom that has emerged as a significant player on the continent. Governed by Chancellor Iriviar under the leadership of High King Salizar, Theze has seen a remarkable transformation from an absolute monarchy to a more peaceful and inclusive state.

Geography

Theze is the third biggest country in the world at 889 000 km², and the second biggest in Meibor. Theze coverers most of the at northern coast and parts of the jungle that lies south of it. The western part has warm fields and is less forested than the rest of country. Theze has a border with Renue to the west and Aldarna to the southwest. The southern part of the country is the northern part of the Najanova Jungle, which covers the central part of the continent. The jungle is cut through by The Yearning, a large river that that meets the sea at the coast city of Naga-Lar. Theze borders Ellios to the southeast and Heksan to the east.

Government

Theze operates as a constitutional monarchy, a system established by High King Salizar after he overthrew his father. The government is now characterized by a balance of power between the monarchy and a parliamentary system, ensuring that the king's authority is moderated by elected representatives. This transition has fostered greater political stability and public participation in governance.
- High King Salizar: The visionary ruler who reformed the kingdom's political structure.
- Chancellor Iriviar: The head of government, responsible for day-to-day administration and policy implementation.
- Parliament: A representative body that collaborates with the monarchy to legislate and govern.

History

Founded only three hundred years ago, Theze rapidly expanded through a series of military campaigns, under the leadership of High King Suraci. Its strategic conquests and unification efforts turned it into the largest nation on Meibor. These campaigns were initially driven by the need for resources and security, but they also fostered a strong national identity.

After Hing King Suraci was assassinated in 1350, his son Abolun took over. Lacking his father's strategic genius, the rate of conquests slowed. Abolun was however determined to set his mark on history, building a grand army at the cost of the nations economy and health. Seeing the risks, young Salizar rose up and deposed his father in 1365

High King Salizar's rise to power marked a pivotal moment in Theze's history. By overthrowing his father, he initiated a series of reforms that transitioned the kingdom from an absolute to a constitutional monarchy. These reforms included:
- Political Reforms: Establishing a parliament and reducing the monarchy's absolute power.
- Social Reforms: Promoting equality and integrating diverse populations, including Yirte communities and refugees fleeing from The Breach.
- Economic Reforms: Modernizing infrastructure and industry to boost economic growth.

Economy

Theze boasts a diverse and thriving economy, supported by its rich natural resources and strategic coastal location. Key exports include:
- Metals: Theze has abundant mineral reserves, including mundane metals like iron, copper, and silver as well magical metals like Orichalcum and Adamantium which are crucial for its industrial and economic growth.
- Crops: Fertile lands support extensive agriculture, producing grains, fruits, and vegetables that sustain both the domestic population and export markets.
- Animal Agriculture: Theze also has many ranches and coops that raise animals like chickens, cows and alpacas.
- Fish: The northern coast provides ample fishing opportunities, supplying a significant portion of the nation's food and export revenue.

Society and Culture

Theze is a melting pot of cultures and species, reflecting its history of conquest and openness to refugees. The predominant species are nagas, but some yirte communities have been absorbed in the nations conquest. Since the breach significant populations of Humans, Gnomes, and Brannur have settled in Theze, contributing to its rich cultural tapestry.
- Diversity: The influx of refugees and immigrants has made Theze a culturally diverse nation, with various traditions, languages, and customs.
- Nobility: While Salizar's reforms have curbed their power, the the noble landowners of Theze still wield considerable influence. Many of them object to the recent actions and have attempted political moves of their own.
- Education: With strong ties to Ishyin Academy and many of its own academies, Theze has a higher number of wizards compared to many other nations, and magical education is highly valued.
- Maritime Trade: Theze's large coastline and port cities like Naga-Lar and Sezei-Lar has led to it have many ships, both military and trade vessels.

Military

The kingdom maintains a powerful military, both to defend its vast territories and to project power. The military includes well trained and equipped naga warriors, wizards trained in Ishyin Academy, and golems produced in spellforges.
