El-Shavri

El-Shavri, located in the northwestern part of Santsim, is an ancient and enigmatic nation. This theocratic kingdom is ruled by the Sun King, whose will is considered divine. The capital of El-Shavri sits on a Salans river delta within the vast Elsam Desert, which covers northeastern Santsim.

El-Shavri is steeped in tradition and secrecy, often keeping to itself and remaining detached from global affairs. Unlike many nations, it accepted few refugees from The Breach. The people of El-Shavri place a high value on soulbinding, and many of its mages are bonded to magical creatures. The shavrian Sun King, in particular, is soul-bound to a legendary Sun Phoenix, which dies and is reborn whenever a new Sun King ascends to power. Additionally, El-Shavri possesses extensive knowledge about Shades and has developed methods to extract secrets from them.

Geography

El-Shavri is the fourth largest country by area in Kastorix and the largest in Santsim at 756 000 km². It owns almost all of the Elsam Desert and small parts of the Ashen Lands and the Tharn mangroves. El-Shavri is bordered to the west by the Tharn commune and by Quarthax to the south. Most of its territory is in the hot Elsam desert so it's cities are either on the river Salans which runs through the country or along the coast.

Government

The government of El-Shavri is an absolute theocracy, with the Sun King holding supreme authority. The Sun King is both the political and spiritual leader, believed to be the earthly representative of the sun deity worshipped by the people. The royal court includes high priests and priestesses who manage various aspects of governance and religious practices.
- Sun King: The divine ruler, whose will is absolute and whose bond with the Sun Phoenix symbolizes the unity of the kingdom and the sun deity.
- High Priests/Priestesses: Influential religious leaders who assist in governing and maintaining the spiritual well-being of the nation.
- Noble Houses: Powerful families that hold significant influence, often through their own soulbound creatures and extensive land ownership.

History

El-Shavri was established as one of the earliest civilizations on Santsim. Its founding myths often involve divine intervention and the establishment of the Sun King's lineage. The kingdom's history is marked by the construction of grand temples, pyramids, and monuments dedicated to their deities.

Throughout its history, El-Shavri has maintained a policy of isolationism, focusing on preserving its traditions and secrets rather than engaging in external conflicts or alliances. This has allowed the nation to develop a unique culture, with ancient practices remaining largely unchanged.

Despite its isolation, El-Shavri has recently faced pressures to modernize and adapt. The need for industrialization and technological advancement has been recognized by some progressive factions within the kingdom, but resistance from traditionalists remains strong. Recently El-Shavri has been supporting other monarchies on the continent like Qual and Terill to curtail the growth of the Glarnast federation, which contests them as the most powerful nation on Santsim.

Economy

El-Shavri's economy is primarily agricultural, supported by the fertile lands of the river delta. The nation is a major exporter of grains, which are highly valued across Santsim.
- Agriculture: The river delta allows for extensive farming, producing surplus grains for export.
- Artisan Goods: While lacking in industrialization, El-Shavri is known for its exquisite handcrafted goods, including textiles, pottery, and jewelry.
- Magical Resources: Although rare, some magical resources are traded, such as phoenix feathers and shade essences.

Society and Culture

El-Shavri is a society deeply rooted in tradition and spirituality. The people hold strong beliefs in the divine right of the Sun King and the power of soulbinding.
- Class System: The society is highly stratified, with a strict class system that dictates one's social status. Advancement is difficult but not impossible, often requiring exceptional talent or divine favor.
- Soulbinding: A revered practice where individuals bond with magical creatures, enhancing their abilities and status. The Sun King's bond with the Sun Phoenix is the most prestigious.
- Magic and Rituals: Magic is an integral part of daily life, with numerous rituals performed to honor the deities and seek their blessings. Temples and sacred sites are abundant, each with specific rituals and purposes.
- Cultural Artifacts: El-Shavri is renowned for its cultural artifacts, including elaborate statues, intricate carvings, and ancient scrolls. These items often hold both artistic and magical significance.

Military

El-Shavri's military is formidable, comprising numerous soldiers and magical creatures. While the nation lacks modern spellforges and runeguns, it compensates with the use of masterwork golems, traditional weapons, and powerful magic.
- Infantry: Well-trained soldiers equipped with traditional weapons and light armor.
- Magical Creatures: Various soulbound creatures, including phoenixes, griffins and sphinxes, used in combat and for defense.
- Golems: Masterwork golems guard the capital and important sites, offering additional security and protection.
