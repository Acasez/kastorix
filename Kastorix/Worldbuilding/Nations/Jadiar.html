Jadiar

Jadiar is a desert nation renowned for its powerful mages and rich magical heritage. Nestled in the vast Jadieran Desert of western Meibor, Jadiar is a beacon for those seeking to master the arcane arts. Its capital, Shanar, stands atop a series of mesa plateaus, where life flourishes around a potent mana node. This unique geography and magical concentration make Shanar a premier destination for studying magic.
While Jadiar is open to all species, the desert heat means few species other than Nagas live there. In recent years, Shanar has a acquired a significant minority population of humans and gnomes due to a series of cooling wards installed throughout the city.

Geography

At 814,000 km², Jadiar is the third largest nation in the world, behind The Draconium and Theze. Most of Jadiar is located in the Jadieran Desert of western Meibor, characterized by scorching hot weather and few sources of water. This causes most of inland Jadiar to be sparsely settled, with a few small cities next to oases and rivers. In the middle of the desert, there are several mesa plateaus, with the capital of Shanar located on the largest one. Shanar is unique due to its large oasis and very potent mana node, making it a rare pearl in the desert and allowed it to grow to be a large city.
The rest of Jadiar's cities sit either in the country's cooler southern part or along the coast. Jadiar has a coastline along Meibor's western edge, giving it a maritime border with Glarnast along the Jasman Strait. It also has two inhabited islands in the strait. Jadiar shares a northeastern border with Thune, an southeastern border with Levia along the Everglow Mountains, and a southern border with Saursi

Government

Jadiar is ruled by an oligarchy of powerful mages, known as the Arcane Conclave. This ruling body is composed of the most influential sorcerers in the nation, each representing different magical disciplines and regions within Jadiar. Membership in the Conclave is earned through magical prowess, contributions to the state, and peer recognition, ensuring that the nation's leadership remains both elite and meritocratic.

History

The harsh Jadieran Desert was largely uninhabited until the legendary sorcerer Arinav discovered the mana-rich plateaus that would become Shanar. Initially a secluded training ground for Arinav and his apprentices, the site gradually attracted other mages, scholars, and merchants. Over centuries, Shanar grew from a rugged outpost into a bustling city known for its magical academies and vibrant marketplace.
For much of its history, Jadiar balanced a complex relationship with Ishyin—both as a rival in magical prowess and as a collaborator in arcane research. While Ishyin's rise has overshadowed Jadiar in recent times, the two nations continue to share knowledge and engage in scholarly exchanges.
In response to overpopulation on the Shanar plateaus and to spur economic growth, the Arcane Conclave has embarked on ambitious projects to make the desert more habitable. These initiatives include:
- Irrigation Canals: Using magic and engineering to divert water and create fertile zones.
- New Settlements: Establishing additional cities and trade routes in previously uninhabitable areas. To encourage settlers, new cooling wards are being set up.
- Mining Expansion: Increasing the extraction of valuable gemstones and magical minerals, such as ember dust, used in fire magic and enchantments.

Economy

Jadiar’s economy thrives on its natural and magical resources. The nation is a leading exporter of:
- Magical Materials: Including rare components for spellcasting and enchanting.
- Gemstones: Particularly Jadieran Jade, prized for its beauty and magical properties.
- Magical Products: Enchanted items, potions, and artifacts crafted by Jadiar's skilled artificers.
Despite limited metal resources, Jadiar's wealth of gemstones and magical substances sustains a robust trade network. Jadiar’s main imports are metals, wood, and food that won't grow in the desert.

Society and Culture

Jadiar’s society is deeply intertwined with magic. From daily life to governance, the influence of sorcery is omnipresent. . Festivals, duels, and public demonstrations of magical prowess are common, reflecting the society's reverence for the arcane.

Military

Jadiar's military is focused on their battlemages, with ordinary soldiers serving to protect them on the battlefield.
