Deshir

Deshir is a monarchy located on the easternmost part of Meibor. It's bordered by Heksan to the northwest and The Mistlands to the southwest. To the northeast Deshir shares a maritime border with the The Shattered Isles, making it the only nation that can easily trade with them.

Geography

Deshir is mostly located on a hilly landscape with few flatlands and a long coastline. Its eastern part is dominated by the Thousand Spires, a series of tall jagged mountains with intense vertical drops. These mountains extend all the way to the sea, with some forming tall isles before fading away. Due to their verticality, these mountains are sparsely populated, but they are home to some of Deshir's famous monasteries.

Deshir's western part is more populated, with the capital city of Lixian and the surrounding cities being quite dense. Deshir has numerous terrace farms on its hillsides, where both rice and potatoes are cultivated. Deshir's southwestern border reaches into the Mistlands, a dangerous area haunted by supernatural mists. Deshir has a series of forts to protect itself from the creatures inside.

Government

While Deshir is a monarchy, the wealthy merchant class holds significant power. The monarch must navigate the interests of these influential merchants, who control much of the trade with the Shattered Isles. The merchant princes have their own council that can propose and veto laws, giving them substantial sway over the kingdom's affairs.

These powers were recently given to the merchants as their influence grew and the government feared a revolution.

History

For a long time, Deshir was quite isolated by the Mistlands and the dense forests. This isolation allowed it to develop a unique culture and strong sense of identity, but it also limited its interactions with neighboring regions.

The discovery of a navigable route to the Shattered Isles marked a turning point in Deshir's history. This newfound connection brought wealth and new ideas, transforming Deshir from a secluded kingdom into a significant player in regional trade. The rise of wealthy merchant families and the expansion of the military to protect these trade routes became defining features of Deshir's modern era.

Economy

Deshir's economy is largely based on agriculture, with terrace farms producing a significant amount of grains such as rice and potatoes. The nation's fertile hillsides and innovative farming techniques have made it a major exporter of these staples.

In recent years, trade has expanded, and some merchants have become wealthy by exporting rare materials from the Shattered Isles to the rest of the continent. This trade includes exotic goods like magical corals, rare minerals, and unique artifacts, which are highly sought after in other nations.

Society and Culture

Deshiran culture, having been long isolated, is distinct and rich. The society places a high value on tradition, spirituality, and self-sufficiency. The monasteries in the Thousand Spires are centers of learning and spiritual guidance, attracting pilgrims and scholars from all over Meibor.

Deshirans are known for their resilience and adaptability, traits honed by living in a challenging environment with frequent supernatural threats from the Mistlands. The arts, particularly poetry, calligraphy, and music, are highly valued and often reflect themes of nature, mysticism, and the mortal condition.

Military

Deshir has a fairly large military, with a standing army only eclipsed by a few world powers. The military is well-trained and experienced, particularly in dealing with supernatural threats from the Mistlands. However, Deshir has yet to industrialize as much as some nations, and its runeguns are behind the rest of the world in terms of technology. Deshir has a small but expanding airship fleet, led by the Sword and Flower.

The military relies heavily on traditional combat techniques and the use of enchanted weapons and armor. Elite units, often trained in the monasteries, specialize in fighting magical creatures and dealing with supernatural phenomena. The military's presence in the Mistlands and the forts along the border are critical to the nation's defense strategy.
