Qual

Qual is a landlocked nation in eastern Santsim, known for its powerful military and many castles. Qual borders Terill to the west, Tharn to the north, Odiana to the south and has a long border with Glarnast to east. In recent years Qual has taken a strong stance against Glarnast and its rapid growth, claiming that it destroys the old ways.
