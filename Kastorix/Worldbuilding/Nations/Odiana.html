Odiana

Odiana is a monarchy on the southeast coast of Santsim known for its cavalry and its warrior queens. Odiana has a strong warrior tradition and long had the largest amount of Brannur outside of Avijor.

Geography

Odiana borders Glarnast to east, Qual and Terill to the north and the Quarthax confederation to west. Odiana lies between the fertile grasslands on the east coast of the continent and the Quarthax Plains on the south coast, and is mostly flat. It's southernmost part is hillier, with a jagged coastlines full of cliffs. The weather is mostly temperate

Government

Odiana is a feudal monarchy with a monarch ruling over smaller duchies. The role of monarch role can be challenged by the dukes and duchesses in a formal battle.

History

Society and Culture

Economy

Odiana trains and sells many mounts and animal products. It has a good working relationship with the tribes of Quarthax and exports many goods gathered from them to rest of the world.

Society and Culture

Military

Odiana has a decently sized professional army that relies heavily on cavalry, using both charges and hit and run tactics. The nation uses a variety of creatures for mounts, including horses, raptors and anzarin, and in rare cases tame wyverns. Recent technological have however developments especially in airships has threatened to reduce dominance of their armies.
