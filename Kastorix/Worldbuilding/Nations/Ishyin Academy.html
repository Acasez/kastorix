Ishyin Academy

Ishyin Academy is the largest and most powerful magical academy in the world, lead by the council of Archmages. While not technically a nation, Ishyin has a lot of political might. Many nations deal with council and Ishyin's many internal factions.

Geography

The academy sits in a hilly region on northwest coast of Meibor. It owns the academy grounds itself and the surrounding area, including the "academy town" and a few villages that supply it with food Ishyin also owns a few islands to the north and northwest, the largest of which is the Archmages Island

There are 8 main spires of the academy, connected by a central platform referred to as floor one. The spires stretch up into the sky, seemingly impossibly high, with bridges and floating platforms spanning between them. The academy sits in a zone of dense mana and the centuries of magic has only intensified what's possible there.

To the south of academy lies the nation of Renue with whom the academy shares close ties.

Government

The academy elects from it's own council from its archmages. While not a set rule, the council has for a long time always had five members who all have equal say on the council.
The current council is composed of;
- Uxima of colors. The colorful many.
- Chaolix the pacifist. The runaway dragon.
- Reitul the phoenix. The first brannur archmage in generations
- Dimmin the curious. The smallest artificer.
- Thara the calm sea. The academy's stormy protector.

History

The academy itself dates back to before even The Draconium, with its lowest floors and libraries full of ancient knowledge but also dangerous monsters and far forgotten traps and wards. During the years the academy has expanded with the wills of its councils and archmages. New floors, spires and wards and enchantments have been added. While some Naga like to claim it to be theirs entirely many species have contributed, though there has almost always been at least one Naga on the council.

Economy

Ishyin is funded primarily by the tuition of its students, which come primarily from neighboring nations of Renue and Theze, but also from all over the world. It also sells many magical goods and works of artifice, as well as magical services like warding, scrying, teleportation and magical consultation. The academy imports a great deal of food from Renue in exchange for cheaper tuition for its citizens. It also imports many magical materials for use in artifice, alchemy, experiments and expanding the towers.

Society and Culture

Ishyin Academy is both a place of learning and an independent institution that serves no country. Magical ability is the highest valued trait there and the academy restricts no types of magic. Due to the students and mages coming there from all over Kastorix, its a very multicultural and multispecies place, and all sorts of people can be found at Ishyin. Some mages spend practically all their lives in the academy, studying, learning and teaching magic.

Military

The academy has a small guarding force of golems, but most of its power comes from its many powerful mages that live there. The academy is also protected by a great deal of wards and magical traps that protects the land and buildings.
