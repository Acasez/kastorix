Geography

Aldarna sits on the western edge of the Najanova jungle, with half of inside and half on the western plains. The Diavar river lies on its western border, with the nation of Thune on the other side. To the northwest of Aldarna lies Renue, to the northeast and east lies Theze and to the south lies the edge of the Everglow Mountains.

Government

Aldarna is ruled by a group of generals, lead by Lajar. Its government has a strong central control on the nation and is known to be harsh on dissidents. Its unclear exactly how much power the other generals in the council has or if Lajar rules alone. He served as one the kingdoms top generals before the coup and was a popular figure.

History

Aldarna is an old nation in the northwest area of Meibor, where the Najanova Jungle meets the northwestern plains. Long an influential kingdom, Aldarnan power declined in the last hundred years, leading to a recent military coup by the nations top general, Lajar. For the last 14 years Aldarna has now been has ruled by a group of generals who oversee the nations. It has seen an investment in artifice

Economy

Aldarna has centralized economy which has undertaken a large modernization campaign recently to connect the country and industrialize it's cities. Aldarna has limited trade with its neighbors, due to political differences. It does however frequently trade with Levia and Jadiar importing metals and magical materials. Aldarna exports wood and food from its jungle

Society and Culture

Aldarna has seen significant cultural shifts in the years after the coup, with its current leadership making efforts to unite the nation under one culture. One initiate has been through the new writing system based on runic designs that is meant to teach children magic and runecraft naturally. Many scholars are skeptical of this idea, but it's too early to tell of its success.

Military

Aldarna has a sizable military for it's size and has spent considerable effort on modernizing it under Lajar's leadership. It features infantry wielding runeguns, artifice and golem support. Aldarna has a core of battlemages that provide support, though unlike it's neighbors, the Aldarnan military is not built around it's mages. Some say its rather the opposite, that Aldarna are developing anti-mage technology to counter its magic centered rivals.
