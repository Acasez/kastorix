Saursi

Saursi is a vibrant nation situated on the eastern coast of Meibor, serving as a crucial trade hub that connects the southeastern and northern parts of the continent. Its strategic location and diverse population make it a melting pot of cultures and commerce.

Saursi

Location: Eastern [[Meibor]]
Size: 195 000 km²
Population: 7 Million
Military Strength: Moderate
Government: Grand duchy
Exports: Wood, food
Imports: Foods
Species: Many nagas, brannur and yirte
History: Old

Geography

Saursi lies below the vast Jadieran Desert, bordering Jadiar and Levia to the north, the Everglow Mountains to the east, and Vern to the south. The terrain is characterized by temperate forests and scrublands, which transition to colder climates further from the desert. This geographical diversity supports a rich ecosystem and abundant natural resources.

Government

Saursi is governed as a grand duchy, with a Grand Duke or Duchess serving as the head of state. The day-to-day administration is overseen by a Chancellor, who implements policies and manages the bureaucracy. The government is structured to balance the interests of the diverse species that call Saursi home, with representation from nagas, brannur, and yirte in the decision-making process.

History

Saursi's origins can be traced back to a coalition of trading posts established by various species seeking to capitalize on the region's strategic location. Over time, these trading posts coalesced into a unified nation, with the grand duchy system emerging as a means of maintaining order and promoting cooperation among the diverse populace.

As trade routes expanded, Saursi grew in importance, becoming a vital link between the northern and southern regions of Meibor. The nation invested in infrastructure, building roads and ports to facilitate the flow of goods and people. This growth attracted more settlers, further enriching Saursi's cultural tapestry and solidifying its role as a trade hub.

Economy

Saursi's economy thrives on trade, with wood and food being major exports. The nation imports metals and gemstones from Levia and Jadiar, utilizing its strategic location as a natural stopping point for caravans and airships. This trade-centric economy has fostered a culture of entrepreneurship and innovation, with bustling markets and a strong merchant class.

Society and Culture

Saursi's society is marked by its diversity and its connection to both the north and south part of Meibor. The nation is also known for its large elephant population, which holds cultural significance and is celebrated in festivals and art. Elephants are seen as symbols of strength, wisdom, and unity, reflecting the values that bind Saursi's diverse communities together.

Military

Saursi maintains a moderate military strength, focused on defending its trade routes and borders. Saursi's military is mostly composed of infantry and cavalry, trained in fighting in its forested environments. The cavalry uses both elephants and raptors alongside ordinary horses for mounts. The military also plays a role in maintaining internal order and protecting the nation's valuable trade interests.
