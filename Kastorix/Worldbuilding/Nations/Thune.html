Thune

Thune is a small republic on the northeastern coast of Meibor along the Diavar Bay, renowned for its airship industry. Despite its modest size, Thune has become a significant player in the global market, producing a considerable portion of the world's airships. which has brought wealth and prestige to the nation in recent years. Thune has invested heavily in airship ports, construction yards, and the recruitment and training of skilled artificers and shipwrights.

Geography

Much of Thune is coastal, bordering the Diavar bay and river. Thune is heavily industrialized and urbanized, with few truly rural areas. There are many cities along the coast, bustling with trade from ports both maritime and aerial. Thune has a maritime border to Renue on the other side of the bay. To the west Thune borders Aldarna across the Diavar river. Southern Thune is less settled, turning into a steppe on the border of the Jadieran Desert and Jadiar.

Government

Thune is a parliamentary republic with elections held every four years. The government is led by a parliament, which elects a president who oversees the executive functions of the state. Additionally, there is a ceremonial first speaker who represents the country in international affairs and formal events.

History

Thune has a long history as a hub of industry, artifice, and trade, situated between Jadiar, the Ishyin Academy, and the western jungles. Its strategic location fostered early growth in commerce and innovation, setting the stage for its current prominence in the airship industry.

In recent decades, Thune has focused its resources on developing its airship construction capabilities. This strategic shift has transformed it from a regional center of trade into a key global player in airship manufacturing. The nation's commitment to advancing its technology and infrastructure has attracted skilled labor and significant investment, further solidifying its status.

Economy

Thune's economy is heavily reliant on its airship industry and exports. The nation's construction yards are among the most advanced in the world, capable of producing fifth-generation airships. The largest of these yards is currently constructing the Suns Glory for El-Shavri, which is set to be the largest airship ever built upon its completion.

To support its airship production, Thune imports a vast array of raw materials from different regions. These include ores from Theze and Levia, crystals and gemstones from Jadiar, magical devices from the Ishyin Academy, and various other materials from across Kastorix. One metal Thune has is Orichalcum which it mines and uses for both it's airship industry and to export.

While some economists have raised concerns about the long-term sustainability of focusing so intensely on a single industry, the current economic boom has undeniably brought prosperity and international recognition to Thune.

Society and Culture

Thune is a melting pot of diverse species, with significant populations of gnomes and nagas. This diversity is reflected in its vibrant cultural scene, with new ideas and trends. The skylines of Thune are tall and striking, filled with airship ports and new buildings. Many different styles of architecture can be found in a blend of different cultures and time periods.

The citizens of Thune take pride in their nation’s technological advancements and their role in the global economy. Competitions and airship unveilings are common, fostering a strong sense of community and national pride. Thune holds a yearly airship race around the country where tinkers and pilots compete.

Military

Thune’s military is relatively small, especially compared to larger nations, but it compensates with an impressive fleet of airships. This fleet provides both defensive capabilities and a means to project power and influence far beyond its borders. The airships are equipped with advanced weaponry and magical enhancements, making them formidable assets in any conflict. Thune is known for it's flagship, Leviathan, which was the first dreadnaught class airship completed.
