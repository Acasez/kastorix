The Windsong Conclave

The Draconium Imperialis is the only surviving nation on Avijor, the northern continent. It is home to almost all of the worlds dragons, as well as most of the dragonkin. The Draconium is an ancient nation formed by draconic clans that stretch back to the times of yore. Since its founding it has been a world power and bastion of stability, wielding a great degree of influence. The most common calendar of the world is the draconic calendar, which starts at the founding of the Draconium. Since The Breach the Draconium has been forced to undergo a lot of changes, to sustain the endless siege from the Void Creatures. The power dynamics between the dragons, dragonkin and other species has shifted.

Geography

At 1 857 000km² the Draconium is the biggest country in the world by area. Most of the country lies on the eastern part of Avijor, covering the easternmost part of the continent. The large island of Revonia (387 000km²) is also part of the Draconium, lying southwest of the mainland. Most of the Draconium is in a temperate region with mild winters and warm summers. The northern parts of the country and western hills can get chillier, but nation rarely sees significant snowfalls.

Before the Breach the Draconium used to border multiple nations to the west, including the territory owned by the world famed Spears Legacy mercenary company. Since the Breach, the nation now only borders territory conquered by the void. The Draconium has always had an well managed series of walls on the borders, but these have been significantly reinforced and modernized since the breach.

The Draconium's capital and largest city of Mellafier lies on the northeastern coast, but it has many other significant cities over its territory. Revonia is less populated than the rest of the country, with a large forest on the east side of the island. The Shattered Isles are not too far to the east of the Revonia, but the The Galeward lies in-between disrupting trade.

Government

The Draconium Imperialis is a oligarchic democracy ruled by an Imperial Senate, which elects its representatives based on regions. Traditionally almost all of the power has been held by the leaders of the various draconic clans that make up the Draconium. The Draconium is, despite its imperial aesthetic, an democracy. Most of the elected officials have historically been dragons, but any could in theory rise into power.

History

Founding

Back in ancient days the dragons and their clans feuded and fought. While a dragon is powerful even now, back then a single dragon was a fearsome foe for even a whole city. But as nations grew and magic, technology and alchemy was developed the power of a dragon waned. And as they spent as much time fighting amongst themselves as they did warring against their enemies, dragons died and the species might have fallen extinct.
It is said that the wisest among them came up the with the idea for the Draconium. A nation of dragons, where they might compete, but not fight. A nation that would harness the dragons tendencies that caused the very strife that almost led to their downfall. Dragons are known to be prideful, possessive and greedy. And the Draconium harnesses those tendencies. A dragon who works in lies and half truths has no pride. A dragon who doesn't care for their people and land doesn't deserve them. And a dragon with no greed will not seek to improve their land and find new things.
Of course setting up the Draconium didn't go perfectly. There were conflicts, there were fights and issues. But dragons don't abandon their projects, and they are proud of their people. A hundred years later the Draconium stood as a beacon of stability amid warring states. So it did thousand years after that. The nation has changed over time, parts of the very rock of the first imperial senate remains.

The Breach

The breach opened 1344 years after the founding of the Draconium. The imperial senate held a meeting a week after. It was ruled to be no ordinary threat and the legions were mobilized. Two more days and the walls almost fell, still. In what later came to be known as the "flight of last hopes", six thousand seven hundred and eighty seven dragons flew to walls and defended it for an entire week. During the siege they felled one void colossi and countless others Void Creatures and managed to push back the void. However he fight claimed more dragons than any other in history, and though it retreated for a bit afterward, the void wasn't anywhere near fully defeated.
The Draconium also took in a unprecedented amount of refugees fleeing the breach then. While some later continued moving to nations in Meibor or Santsim, many settled permanently. While the Draconium had always accepted other species than dragons and dragonkin, they had always been small minorities before.
30 years after the breach and the Draconium has changed drastically. Artifice and wizardry has become far more widespread. New spellforges, academies and alchemical laboratories are opening. The Draconium offers a scholarship to students at Ishyin Academy that agree to serve as battlemage in the legions for four years after graduating. The imperial legions have had their weapons and tactics altered to face this new threat. And the nation now presents itself as Kastorix's last hope on the world stage. A bastion against the void. Economy

The Draconium Imperialis boasts a wealthy and sophisticated economy, underpinned by a variety of factors that have allowed it to amass significant wealth over the centuries.
The Draconium's vast mines are rich in precious metals such as gold, silver, and platinum, as well as high-quality marble and sandstone. Their mining industry is second only to the naga nations. The Draconium's also produce some of the world's finest silk, wines, and other agricultural products on their plantations. The Draconium produces a wide range of manufactured goods, from everyday items like clothes and tools to luxury items such as glassware and finely crafted compasses. Their craftsmanship is renowned, contributing to the high demand for their products.

Infrastructure

The Draconium's well-maintained roads and transport systems facilitate efficient movement of goods across the nation, boosting internal trade and economic stability. It's stability and growth has given it large, well structured harbors, warehouses and internal markets.

Financial Services

One of the most unique aspects of the Draconium's economy is its sophisticated banking system. Stemming from their historical stability and vast hoards, dragons pioneered financial services in Avijor. They provided banking services, loans, and financial advice to other nations, securing a dominant position in regional finance. The Draconium's wealth and stability made it a reliable financial center, with extensive investments both domestically and internationally

Post-Breach Economic Changes

Since the breach, much of the Draconium's economy has been redirected to support its defense efforts against the void creatures. This has led to the nation running a deficit for the first time in centuries. Despite the increased expenditure, the Draconium's vast hoards mean it could sustain this deficit for over a century. However, the economic landscape is evolving rapidly, with new spellforges, academies, and alchemical laboratories being established to support both the war effort and future economic diversification.

Society and Culture

The Draconium is known for many proud traditions and for its strict, but fair culture. It's many regions and the draconic clans that live there have differences, but the nation has a strong united culture.
The worship of the Primordial Dragons is the main religion in the Draconium and their temples can be found in many cities, but the religion has little impact in the day to day life of most of it's citizens.

Military

Draconic Legions

The draconic legions have been famous throughout the eras for their numbers and discipline. They have fought with spears and shields, halberds and crossbows and are now armed with runeguns and long pikes. Their iconic orange-painted armor distinguishes them in battle. Presently, all legions are deployed to defend the walls against the relentless onslaught of Void Creatures. The legions coordinate closely with artillery units, battlemages, and dragons, utilizing modified runeguns to counter the unique threats posed by the void.

Dragons

Dragons are the Draconium's most formidable asset. Their natural abilities—flight, speed, armored scales, and powerful physical and magical attacks—make them unparalleled on the battlefield. Dragons often serve as commanders and leaders within the legions, though many are not averse to engaging directly in combat. Given their slow growth and long lifespans, the Draconium prioritizes the protection of their dragons. Since the breach and the historic Flight of Last Hopes, an increasing number of dragons have joined the army, bolstering the nation's defenses with their might.
The most powerful dragons are part of the Draconic Champions, a honored title given those with incredible combat feats, who serve as an elite unit defending dragonkind from all enemies.

Mages

Historically, the Draconium had few mages due to the dragons' dominance and self-sufficiency in combat. However, the breach has necessitated a change and now new academies train battlemages, and students from Ishyin Academy receive scholarships in exchange for military service. Given their limited numbers, mages primarily serve in support roles, providing crucial fire support and altering battlefield conditions. Zaesna, the nation's ambassador at Ishyin and a veteran legionnaire, also teaches battlefield magic at the academy as many mages are not just to working with armies.

Artillery

The Draconium maintains a robust artillery division, featuring runegun cannons, enchanted ballista's, hwachas, and other experimental weaponry. Given the defensive nature of the current conflict, significant resources have been allocated to developing and enhancing these weapons to better combat the void creatures.

Airships

Similar to their historical reluctance to develop a large cadre of mages, the Draconium was slow to adopt airship technology. Dragons' natural flight abilities made airships seem redundant. However, the need for versatile aerial support has led to the rapid expansion of their airship fleet. The fleet now includes many smaller vessels, led by one of the world's largest airships, The Dragons Roar, which is large enough to carry dozens of dragons on board.

The Wings of Truth

The Wings of Truth serve as the Draconium's military intelligence agency and, according to some, its secret police. Known for its efficiency and uncanny ability to be where it is most needed, the Wings of Truth plays a critical role in maintaining internal security. With the majority of the military focused on defense, the Wings of Truth monitor for saboteurs and spies, ensuring the nation's stability. They are also notable for their use of dragon riders, a rarity in the broader military structure.
