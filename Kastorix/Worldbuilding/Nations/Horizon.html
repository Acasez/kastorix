Horizon

Horizon is an isolated island nation in the northern part of the Grand Sea, located north of The Shattered Isles and northeast of The Draconium. An absolute dictatorship under a mysterious figure known only as The Overseer, Horizon is known is known for their reluctance to trade with the rest of the world and their powerful Horizon Knights who acts as guardians and spies for the nation. Its also known for its advanced technology in some fields and knowledge of lost ruins

Geography

The country of Horizon comprises the island of the same name alongside a few smaller isles. The island of Horizon is fairly large (303,112 km²) and has a rounded shape. Its location in the Grand Sea makes it cold and stormy. Much of the island is covered in taiga forests with hardy trees, and it also has numerous mountains and cliffs.

The island is believed to contain one or more ruins from The Remnant, the mysterious civilization that left ruins on multiple parts of Kastorix.

Government

Horizon is a dictatorship ruled by an enigmatic figure known as The Overseer. Very little is known about The Overseer to the outside world, including their species, age, and gender. Due to its strict laws and lack of diplomacy, little else is known about how the island's government operates. Some believe the that Overseer wields considerable magical power and possesses ancient knowledge from the Remnant civilization.

History

Much of Horizon's history is shrouded in mystery. It is an ancient nation, with origins possibly dating back to the era of the Remnant civilization. Legends speak of Horizon as a place of refuge and knowledge, where the remnants of ancient magic and technology have been preserved. Its isolation has kept it largely untouched by the outside world, allowing it to develop in unique ways.

Economy

Horizon does minimal trade with other nations. It is unclear how exactly they sustain such a large civilization on the cold island. Some theorize the use of mana-powered greenhouses and advanced agricultural techniques to support their population. Despite their self-sufficiency, Horizon is known to possess advanced technological and magical artifacts, suggesting a highly developed internal economy focused on innovation and self-reliance.

Society and Culture

Little is known about the culture of Horizon. It is believed to be a highly regimented society, with strict laws and a focus on duty and loyalty to The Overseer. The Horizon Knights are a central aspect of their society, revered as protectors and enforcers. The secrecy surrounding Horizon extends to its culture, with few outsiders ever gaining a glimpse of life within its borders.

Military

Horizon’s most famous military units are the Horizon Knights, who serve as scouts, guardians, and spies. Horizon Knights wear heavy armor of a strange interlocking design and carry enchanted melee weapons, which they use alongside magic in combat. They are most known for their skills in Teleportation magic, both long-distance and combat teleports. Horizon Knights are believed to be at roughly magus level in magical proficiency, marking them as skilled battlemages.

In most cases, Horizon Knights are the only military personnel sent away from the island, creating the illusion that all their soldiers are that strong. However, they are the nation's elite troops. Sometimes Horizon squires, who are training to be knights, can be seen on missions. The main military force of Horizon is likely well-equipped and highly trained, with a focus on advanced tactics and magical warfare.
