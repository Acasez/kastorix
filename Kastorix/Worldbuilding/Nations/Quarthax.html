Quarthax

Quarthax is a loose federation of different migratory tribes that inhabit the plains and steppes of southern Santsim. Most of these tribes follow traditional ways that have been passed down for thousands of years, moving around as their herds do. The tribes use horses, anzarin, and sometimes plains raptor mounts, herding a wide array of animals. The different tribes are diverse, with distinct traditions, equipment, trade practices, and homelands. Quarthax is the name of a communal meeting place and event held every other year, where most of the tribes gather to discuss matters, trade, and celebrate their culture.

Geography

The tribes of Quarthax are located on the southern half of Santsim, south of the highlands. They are located southeast of El-Shavri and west of Terill and Odiana. The territory Quarthax is based on is dominated by steppes and plains, where farming is hard, but migratory herds thrive. The southmost part of Quarthax territory follows the rocky southern coast of Santsim, and the tribes that visit often fish in the waters. Along the central part of the coast lies a boreal forest, that's often visited by the tribes.

Government

The Quarthax is a meeting place and defensive alliance, not a formal government. It has little impact on the day-to-day affairs of the tribes, which each maintain their own forms of leadership. Leadership styles vary among the tribes, from chieftains and councils of elders to more democratic assemblies.

History

The origins of Quarthax date back thousands of years, when the first nomadic tribes began to settle the plains and steppes of southern Santsim. These early tribes formed loose alliances for mutual protection and resource sharing, setting the foundation for the Quarthax federation.
Over centuries, as external threats and opportunities for trade increased, the tribes of Quarthax began to formalize their gatherings. The biannual meeting known as the Quarthax became a central event, fostering unity among the tribes and enabling them to present a united front against invaders. This period also saw the development of the tribes' famed cavalry tactics, which have become a cornerstone of their military strategy.

Economy

The tribes' primary exports are animal products and mounts. These include hides, foodstuffs, clothing, and crafts made from animal materials. Many tribes also sell high-quality horses and anzarins to neighboring nations. Some tribes specialize in gathering rare ingredients or hunting magical creatures. The Quarthax meeting provides an excellent opportunity for trade between the tribes, but they also engage in trade whenever possible with other tribes and nations. Tribes like the Djangarin focus on trading and have established networks with other nations on Santsim.

Society and Culture

As nomadic tribes, the people of Quarthax are on the move most of the time, and they have few permanent structures. Their culture is rich in oral traditions, music, and dance, with each tribe maintaining its own unique customs and rituals. Festivals and ceremonies are important cultural events, often held during the Quarthax gatherings. These gatherings are also times for arranging marriages, settling disputes, and forging new alliances.

Military

Quarthax is famed for its cavalry. Their mounted archers are well known for their skill and precision, and some tribes have begun to incorporate runeguns into their arsenal. The tribes use hit-and-run tactics and guerrilla warfare to great effect, often surprising and outmaneuvering their enemies. Despite their formidable ground forces, some tribes have expressed concerns about their vulnerability to airships and are discussing potential strategies to counteract aerial threats.
