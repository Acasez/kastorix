Void Colossi

PHY: 6
DEX: 1
INT: 6
WIL: 8
Health: 86
Mana: 256
Aura: 256

Saves

Fortitude: 14 Reflex: 5 Will: 14

Others

Resistances: Physical 15, Elemental 10

Speeds: Fly 5

Actions

Anti-Magic Pulse ()

Requirement: 1 Minute Cooldown, 30 Mana

Effect: The Void Colossi emits a pulse of void antimagic. Any creatures within 8 tiles that are maintaining or sustaining a spell need to make a WIL save against 6 + WIL + d20 or lose their spell.

Grasping Barrage ()

Effect: The Void Colossi makes up to five Vine Grasp attacks. It can only target each creature with a max of two of these attacks.

Null Beam ()

Requirement: 1 Minute Cooldown, 35 Mana

Effect: The Void Colossi fires a beam of of unraveling energy in a 15 tile long, 3 tiles wide line. Creatures in the line need to make a Reflex save or take 10d6 force damage.

Passives

Aura Drain

Effect: Whenever the Void Colossi uses Vine Grasp and hits a creatures aura it deals an additional 2d6 damage and regains that much aura.

Otherworldly Presence

Effect: Any creature that enters or starts their turn within 30 tiles of the Void Colossi and can see it needs to make a WIL save against DC 24 or be frightened 1 (2 on a critical failure). On a success they are immune to this effect for 24 hours.

Void Form

Effect: The Void Colossi has advantage on saving throws with the Fear, Mind or Soul traits

Weapons

Vine Grasp: 2d8 Slashing (Traits: Agile,Disarm)
Void Blast: 3d10 Force (Traits: Ranged,Volley)
