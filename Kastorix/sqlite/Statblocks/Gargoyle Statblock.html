Gargoyle

Elemental, Earth

PHY: 4
DEX: -1
INT: -2
WIL: 1
Health: 17
Mana: 18
Aura: 18

Saves

Fortitude: 8 Reflex: 1 Will: 3

Others

Resistances: Slashing 5, Physical 2, Fire 2,

Speeds: Walk 3, Fly 3

Senses: Perception 5

Skills: Athletics +6, Stealth +8

Actions

Shape Rock ()

Requirement: The gargoyle is within 3 tiles to earthen ground or walls.

Effect: The gargoyle shapes the earth into a rock that holds within its claws, allowing it to use the Throw Rock Strike

Tremor ()

Effect: The gargoyle slams into the ground, creating rough terrain in adjacent tiles and forcing all creatures there to make Fortitude saves or become prone

Weapons

Claw: 1d8 Slashing (Traits: Agile)
Throw Rock: 1d8 Bludgeoning (Traits: Ranged,Propulsive)
