Actions

Fierce Dive ()

Requirement: The Wyvern rider is mounted on a wyvern that's airborne and not immobilized.

Effect: The wyvern dives, Flying twice to end up at a lower elevation than it started. At the end of the movement the rider can make a Glaive attack with a +2 bonus.

Act Together ()

Requirement: Once per turn

Effect: The Rider and Wyvern both takes 1 action.

Evasive Maneuver ()

Requirement: The Wyvern rider is mounted on a wyvern that's airborne and not immobilized. Once per minute

Effect: The Rider commands the Wyvern to dodge. The Rider and the Wyvern get a +2 bonus to Reflex saves until the start of its next turn. The Wyvern Flies.
