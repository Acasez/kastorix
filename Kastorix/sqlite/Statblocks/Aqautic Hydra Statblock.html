Aqautic Hydra

Beast

PHY: 5
DEX: 2
INT: -3
WIL: 2
Health: 33
Mana: 19
Aura: 19

Saves

Fortitude: 11 Reflex: 4 Will: 4

Others

Speeds: Swim 6, Land 3

Senses: +6 Perception, Low Light Vision

Skills: Athletics +7,

Actions

Trample ()

Effect: The hydra strides twice. It makes a Stomp attack against any creature it moves over as part of this movement.

Biting Frenzy ()

Requirement: 1d4 turn cooldown

Effect: The hydra makes one Bite attack for each head it has. Attacks made against unique targets only increase the MAP by 2.

Always more heads ()

Requirement: The hydra starts its turn with a head destroyed but not cauterized and less than 9 heads.

Effect: The Hydra spends 7 mana. It's destroyed head grows into to two more.

Passives

Hydra Regeneration

Effect: The Hydra spends 1 mana each turn to regenerate 20 health (no action) spread among its body and heads. It cannot use this ability if it took fire*, lightning or acid damage last turn. *Heat damage (fire damage while underwater) does not disable the regeneration.

Reactive Heads

Effect: The Hydra has one additional reaction for making reactive strikes for each head it has.

Hydra Heads

Effect: The Hydra starts with 5 heads, which can be targeted as Precise strike with a +2 defense bonus. Each head has 9 health. If a head is destroyed and not cauterized with fire, acid and lightning it can be restored as usual. If all the heads are destroyed and it cannot grow any back, the hydra dies.

Weapons

Bite: 2d6 Piercing (Traits: Grab,Agile)
Stomp: 2d6 Bludgeoning (Traits: Deadly)
