Frost Wyvern

Beast, Dragonoid

PHY: 4
DEX: 3
INT: -2
WIL: 1
Health: 22
Mana: 12
Aura: 12

Saves

Fortitude: 8 Reflex: 7 Will: 3

Others

Resistances: Cold 8, Physical 3

Speeds: Fly 6, Land 4

Senses: Perception 8

Skills: Athletics 6, Brawling 6, Survival 5

Actions

Freezing Breath ()

Requirement: 5 Mana, 1d4 turn cool-down

Effect: The Wyvern breaths out a 5 tile cone of freezing air. All creatures caught in the blast make a Fortitude save against d20 + PHY + 3 or 2d6 cold damage

Breath in ()

Effect: The dragon breathes in. If it uses Freezing Breath next turn it costs 3 less mana and deals one d6 more damage. If Freezing Breath is on cooldown next turn it instead reduces the cooldown by one

Weapons

Claw: 2d6 Slashing (Traits: Agile)
Bite: 2d8 Piercing
Tail: 1d12 Bludgeoning (Traits: Shove)
