Feat NameDescription
AcolyteYou've served in a temple or church.
You're trained in religion lore and crafting, society or insight. You gain the Skilled feat.
You start with 7 gold and a holy symbol
Airship PilotYou've trained to pilot airships and navigate the skies.
You're trained in airship lore and crafting, arcana or survival. You gain the Expert Pilot feat.
You start with 5 gold and an airship piloting license
ArtisanYou have trained in a craft or trade.
You are trained in crafting and sleight of hand, society or persuasion and gain the Skilled feat.
You start with a set of artisan's tools and 10 gold pieces.
CriminalYou have stayed on the other side of the law.
You are trained in sleight of hand or stealth and get the Sneaky feat.
You are also trained in Underworld Lore. You start with 8 gold.
EntertainerYou make money performing, making music or dancing.
You are trained in performance or athletics, one instrument of your choice and have the Quick Witted feat.
You start with an instrument of your choice and 12 gold pieces.
ExplorerYou have traveled to many different places and seen many different things.
You are trained in survival or nature and gain the Surveyor feat.
You start with a set of traveler's clothes, a bedroll, and 8 gold pieces.
HunterYou've learned how to hunt using traditional ranged weapons or runeguns.
You are trained in either bows, crossbows or runeguns.
You are also trained in nature or survival.
You gain the Marksman feat.
You start with a ranged weapon that you are trained in and 10 gold pieces.
LabourerYou have been working hard, tough jobs and can take on physical strain other mages might not
You're trained in athletics and crafting, survival or brawling. You gain the Defensive Training feat.
You start with 5 gold and set of sturdy tools
MedicYou are practiced in the art of healing and curing.
You are trained in medicine or athletics and get the Battlefield Medic feat.
You start with 5 gold and a medicine kit
NobleYou come from a wealthy or influential family.
You are trained in society, insight or persuasion and gain the Skilled feat.
You start with 15 gold pieces and have a seal from your house.
ScholarYou have spent years studying a specific field of knowledge.
You are trained in arcana, history, or nature and gain the Dedicated Student feat.
You start with a book on your field of study and 5 gold pieces.
SoldierYou have trained and fought in a army or militia.
You are trained in athletics or brawling and gain the Army Training feat.
You start with a set of light armor and a martial weapon of your choice (worth at most 10 gold)
SpyYou have worked in the shadows, gathering information and secrets.
You are trained in stealth or deception and gain the Sneaky feat.
You start with a disguise kit, a forged identity and 10 gold pieces.
