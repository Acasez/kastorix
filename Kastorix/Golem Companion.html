Golem Companion Rules

Golem companions can be built by taking the Golemcrafter arcane feat. It allows you to make a golem companion to follow you along on your adventures. When you create your golem, choose a model for it to take.

Golem companions have health equal to (3 x your level) + your INT + its PHY. Choose a model at the end of this page for its starting stats. As golems aren’t living creatures it does not have an Aura. Your golem takes no actions on its own and goes on initiative. Each round, you can use any of your actions (including reactions and free actions) for yourself or your golem as long as it's within 10 tiles of you. Your golem is trained in natural weapons and potentially other skills based on its model. Its proficiency increases with feats.

Golem Upgrades can be found at the bottom of the page

Golem Models

Brawler

Your golem is built with inbuilt weapons and protection, making it tough and dangerous. Its blunt arms cannot hold delicate objects or manipulate things. It has resistance 6 to physical damage and 4 tiles movespeed.
It has a natural slam weapon, +5 (PHY + trained), 1d10 + PHY bludgeoning damage. It’s trained in brawling and fortitude saving throws

PHY DEX INT WIL
+4 0 -3 -5

Soldier

Your golem is built as a humanoid soldier. It’s versatile and capable of holding weapons and shields in its arms. It has resistance 2 to physical damage and 4 tiles movespeed.
It’s trained in simple weapons, shields and one weapon group of your choice. It’s trained in two of these skills, athletics, brawling, intimidation and stealth. It’s also trained in reflex saving throws

PHY DEX INT WIL
+2 +2 -3 -5

Quadruped

Your golem is built as a quadruped form, resembling an animal of some sort. It cannot pick up objects or interact with things. It has resistance 4 to physical damage and 6 tiles movespeed.
It has a natural stomp weapon, +5 (PHY + trained), 1d8 + PHY bludgeoning damage. It’s trained in athletics, and fortitude or reflex saving throws

PHY DEX INT WIL
+3 +1 -3 -5

Scout

Your golem is built as a nimble scout, trading resistance for speed and dexterity. It has a 5 tiles movespeed, ignores difficult terrain and treats greater difficult terrain as difficult terrain
It’s trained in simple weapons and one weapon group of your choice. It’s trained in perception and can use your INT as its modifier when making perception checks.
It’s also trained in two of these skills, acrobatics, performance sleight of hand or stealth. Finally it's trained in reflex saving throws

PHY DEX INT WIL
+1 +3 -3 -5
