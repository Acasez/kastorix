V1.2 - 20th July

Game Updates

  • Changed the Mana and Aura gained by level to 3 instead of 4, but also decreased the mana cost of spells per spell rank to 3. This lowers Aura health a bit, decreasing durability of player characters and mages, and making the Aura Block reaction less powerful.
  • Lowered the damage of some spells and attacks to compensate.
  • Codified rules for Mana Transferring for when you need to send mana to someone quickly and don't care for the risk of mana poisoning.
  • Added rules for Soulbonding and soulbonded creatures.
  • Added rules and descriptions for Lore skills (beyond "they work like in pathfinder").
  • Made the four great destructive spells (Earthquake, Inferno, Tornado and Tsunami) take more than 3 actions, but gave them wilder and more powerful effects to cement their role as climactic setpieces.
  • New Content
    • Artificers Weapons for unique weapons crafted with the feat of the same name.
    • A series of arcane feats focused on Mana Threads to connect with targets and cast touch spells at range.
    • A series of duelist feats focused on parrying.
    • More new feats like; Quip, Academic, Expanded Soulsight
    • New spells like; Glide, Magnetic Field, Quick Jet, Earthen Launch, Wave Dash, Thunder Rush

Site Updates

  • Updated the RPG Overview page, again with cool CSS improvements to make it clearer and more visually appealing
  • Added action boxes that describe custom actions on different parts of the site
  • Improved this Patch Notes page to be stylish and readable.

V1.1 - 14th April

Game Updates

  • Added focus points as a standard non-magical resource. Gain 1 per fourth level, regain at 10 mins rest, use them for a wide variety of actions and to empower stuff.
  • Redesigned how stat increases work. You now simply get to increase one stat every 3 levels, though you can't raise the same stat twice in a row or go above 5 in a stat. Advantages that used to give stat buffs have been redesigned.
  • Removed the old system for specific spellshaping failure, it didn't add much and was a lot of work per spell.
  • Instead we have rules for upcasting spells to higher ranks for many spells.
  • Added Runegun upgrades. Unleash your inner gun nut and customize your weapon.
  • Added many more Combat Maneuvers and updated them to hopefully be more readable.
  • Made Off-Guard a numeric condition that gives -X to all saves. Enjoy being Off-Guard 3 while restrained. (But no, it still doesn't stack)
  • Made ranged weapons use range increments. Base ranges are reduced, but you can now take shots from really far distances at increasing penalties.
  • New Content
    • Capacitor upgrades for Golems, project magic from your armored walkers
    • Tactical Combat Maneuvers, get your allies in and out of danger
    • Lots of new feats such as; Enhancement Brewer, Runed Ammo, Expert Pilot
    • Gadgets and Items like; Orb of Unweaving, Atlatls, Shurikens
    • New spells like; Soul Ward, Slip, Inflame, Orbiting Earth, Propel, Shunt

Site Updates

  • Updated the RPG Overview page, it's no longer outdated and the phrasing should be better
  • Updated the DM Screen, its save and modal system is no longer running on its own code, but a variant on the character sheet using the same functions so updates should be easier for both of them.
  • Rewrote the custom weapon logic, it now works again and could also be used in the character sheet if you get a custom weapon.
