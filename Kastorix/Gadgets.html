Gadgets

List of all gadgets and their effects

Gadgets are a shorthand for various magical trinkets and tools crafted by artificers. Gadgets can be gotten by some feats or acquired in other ways, though simply buying them is often harder than it might appear. Gadgets can only be used a limited amount of times per day, as they need to recover mana over time or be reconstructed.

Gadget Types

Gadgets are split into five types; bombs, equipment, held items, thrown devices and upgrades to other gadgets. If you need to make a roll for a gadget the modifier is your Crafting skill + your DEX

Bombs

Bombs are gadgets that are thrown to cause an instant effect in an aura. You can throw bombs 3 + your DEX or PHY tiles.

Equipment

Equipment are gadgets that are worn. You cannot wear gadgets in the same equipment slot (no two boot gadgets) or where you have other permanently enchanted items. Equipped items don’t interfere with your armor. They can be activated with an action, unless specified otherwise.

Held Items

Held Items are gadgets that need to be held to be used.

Thrown Devices

Thrown Devices are gadgets that are thrown to cause a lingering effect in an aura around them. You can throw them 3 + your DEX tiles. Their effect lasts for a minute, until you deactivate them as an action or until they are destroyed.

Upgrades

Upgrades aren’t new gadgets, but rather modifications on old ones. They modify one or more gadgets.
